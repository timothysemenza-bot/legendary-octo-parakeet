What Is Included

  • Kajabi Information Architecture Cleanup: simplify navigation and align public pages, membership entry, and member area experience.
  • Join Flow Optimization: remove fragile coupon-based workarounds and implement a clear free-member onboarding path.
  • Events + Communications Operations: standardized event publishing, reminder workflows, and newsletter template setup.
  • Integration Pass: clean connection points for Zoom and donation/payment paths (including Zeffy where applicable).
  • Handover Enablement: practical SOP checklist and training session for Jessica plus one backup admin.

Constraints and Clarifications

  • Donated phase scope is fixed: Kajabi optimization only in this initial engagement.
  • Out of scope for this phase: custom platform rebuild, advanced CRM migrations, and major rebrand work.
  • Access dependency: timely admin access to Kajabi, Wix, domain/DNS, Zoom, and relevant tools.
  • Revision boundary: up to two revision rounds on core page and automation templates.
  • Timeline dependency: delays in approvals or access pause the delivery clock.

Success Criteria (Initial Phase)

  • JWBLNG has one clear member journey from discovery to onboarding to program participation.
  • Event setup and member communications are repeatable for a non-technical internal team.
  • Kajabi becomes the primary operating platform, reducing platform confusion and workflow overhead.
  • Leadership has a stable baseline plus clear options for any paid follow-on support.

Immediate Next Steps

  • Board Approval: JWBLNG leadership confirms approval for the donated 3-week Kajabi optimization phase.
  • Platform Access: provide Timmy with admin access to Kajabi, Wix, Zoom, Zeffy, and domain/DNS controls.
  • Platform Consolidation Decision: confirm Kajabi as the primary platform and define what remains on or is retired from Wix.
  • Kickoff: implementation starts immediately once approvals and access are complete.
