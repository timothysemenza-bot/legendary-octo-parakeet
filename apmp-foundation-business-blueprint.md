# APMP BD Lifecycle-Founded Business Blueprint

This business model uses APMP’s **Introduction to the Business Development Lifecycle** as the core operating spine, not as the totality of the APMP Body of Knowledge (BOK).

## 1) Foundation Principle

Use the 8-phase APMP business development lifecycle as the base system:
1. Market identification
2. Account planning and positioning
3. Opportunity assessment (identification and qualification)
4. Opportunity/capture planning
5. Proposal planning
6. Proposal development
7. Negotiation and post-submittal activity
8. Delivery and ongoing customer relationships

Your consultancy is built to improve client performance at each phase with clear gates, roles, artifacts, and KPIs.

## 2) How APMP Is Applied

### What this model uses as the foundation
- End-to-end lifecycle discipline
- Gate decisions (especially bid/no-bid and pursuit validation)
- Positioning before proposal writing
- Customer-focused strategy transfer from capture to proposal
- Lessons learned and continuous improvement loops

### What this model does not claim
- It does not attempt to replicate or replace the full APMP BOK.
- It does not require clients to implement every APMP method at once.
- It prioritizes practical lifecycle execution first, then expands into deeper APMP practices as maturity grows.

## 3) Business Positioning

Build a **BD lifecycle and proposal performance consultancy** for B2B and regulated-market organizations that need better win rates, less rework, and predictable growth.

Primary outcomes sold:
- Better qualified pipeline
- Better pursuit decisions
- Better proposal quality and submission reliability
- Better post-submittal and delivery positioning for recompete/expansion

## 4) Lifecycle-Aligned Service Architecture

1. **Lifecycle Diagnostic (2 weeks)**
- Baseline current-state process against the 8 phases
- Identify bottlenecks, weak gates, and role confusion
- Deliver 90-day remediation roadmap

2. **Gate and Capture System Setup (4–6 weeks)**
- Build bid/no-bid scorecard and governance cadence
- Implement account planning and capture plan templates
- Add competitive and Price-to-Win checkpoints

3. **Proposal Operations Build (4–8 weeks)**
- Proposal planning package (kickoff, storyboard, compliance matrix, content plan)
- Proposal development controls (owners, schedule, review standards, final document review)
- Submission and contingency protocols

4. **Post-Submittal and Delivery Value Loop (retainer)**
- Clarification/negotiation response support
- Win/loss and lessons-learned integration into CRM and knowledge base
- Delivery-to-recompete relationship playbook

## 5) ICP and Buyer Profile

Prioritize organizations that:
- Pursue at least 8 to 10 formal opportunities per year
- Have inconsistent pursuit discipline or proposal fire drills
- Need to improve win performance without building a large internal team

Economic buyers and champions:
- CRO / VP Sales / Chief Growth Officer
- Head of Proposals / Capture Lead
- COO / GM / Managing Director

## 6) Operating Model (Your Internal Delivery Standard)

- Every engagement maps client activity to the 8 lifecycle phases.
- Every phase has a gate with explicit exit criteria.
- No proposal development starts without a qualified opportunity and approved pursuit strategy.
- All pursuits end with lessons learned captured and fed back to account/capture plans.
- Delivery teams are measured on client behavior change, not just document output.

## 7) KPI Stack

Lifecycle health KPIs:
- Market-to-qualified-opportunity conversion rate
- Bid/no-bid decision cycle time
- Qualified opportunity win probability at gate entry
- Proposal compliance defect rate
- On-time submission rate
- Clarification turnaround time post-submittal
- Win rate by segment and deal type
- Recompete/expansion revenue from delivered accounts

Business KPIs:
- Gross margin per engagement
- Retainer renewal rate
- Client expansion rate
- Time-to-value (days to first measurable process improvement)

## 8) Revenue Model

- Diagnostic: fixed fee
- Setup/implementation: milestone-based fixed fee
- Ongoing support: monthly retainer with capacity limits
- Enablement: cohort or seat-based training

Example packaging ladder:
- Lifecycle Diagnostic: $4k to $10k
- Lifecycle System Build: $20k to $75k
- Managed BD/Proposal Support: $8k to $35k per month

## 9) Team and Tools

Core team:
- Principal consultant (strategy and executive advisory)
- Capture/proposal lead
- Proposal writer/analyst
- Fractional pricing, design, or compliance support as needed

Core stack:
- CRM for account and opportunity lifecycle visibility
- PM/workflow for pursuit and proposal execution
- Content and evidence library for reuse and governance
- KPI dashboard tied to lifecycle phases and gate outcomes

## 10) 12-Month Build Path

1. **Q1:** Validate lifecycle diagnostic + 2 to 3 pilot clients
2. **Q2:** Standardize gate framework and proposal operations package
3. **Q3:** Add sector-specific variants (public, healthcare, enterprise B2B)
4. **Q4:** Productize lifecycle retainer and establish predictable recurring revenue
