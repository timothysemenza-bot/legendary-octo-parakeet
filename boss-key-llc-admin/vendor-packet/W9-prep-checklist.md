# W-9 Prep Checklist (Boss Key LLC)

Use this before sending your W-9.

## Form fields to confirm

1. Name (as shown on tax return): `BOSS KEY LLC`
2. Business name/disregarded entity name:
   - leave blank unless you are using a different DBA
3. Federal tax classification:
   - `Individual/sole proprietor or single-member LLC` (default single-member LLC)
4. Exemptions:
   - typically blank for your setup unless advisor says otherwise
5. Address:
   - `1596 Jason Dr, Cinnaminson, NJ 08077` (or your current official business mailing address)
6. Requester name/address:
   - client fills this if they want
7. Taxpayer Identification Number:
   - use EIN: `41-4426404`
8. Signature + date:
   - sign as `Timothy Charles Semenza, Sole Member`

## Quality check before send

1. EIN is correct and legible.
2. Legal name exactly matches IRS/NJ records.
3. Address is current.
4. Signed and dated.
5. PDF filename is clean: `W9-Boss-Key-LLC.pdf`

## Security rules

1. Do not post W-9 in public channels.
2. Send only to verified AP/procurement contacts.
3. Keep local + cloud backups of final signed W-9.
