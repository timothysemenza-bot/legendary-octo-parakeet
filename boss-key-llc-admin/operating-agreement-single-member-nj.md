The Company is member-managed. The Member has full authority to manage and control the Company, including entering contracts, opening bank accounts, borrowing funds, and taking other actions reasonably required to conduct Company business.

The Member may contribute cash, property, or services to the Company as determined by the Member. Additional contributions are at the Member's discretion.

All profits and losses shall be allocated to the Member. Distributions may be made to the Member at times and in amounts determined by the Member, subject to applicable law and Company obligations.

## 10. Tax Classification

Unless otherwise elected, the Company will be treated as a disregarded entity for federal and state income tax purposes.

## 11. Records and Accounting

The Company shall maintain complete and accurate books and records, including:

1. Formation and legal documents
2. Financial records
3. Tax returns and filings
4. Material contracts

The fiscal year of the Company shall end on December 31 unless changed by the Member.

## 12. Liability and Indemnification

The Member shall not be personally liable for Company debts, obligations, or liabilities except as required by law or by separate written agreement.

To the fullest extent permitted by law, the Company shall indemnify the Member for acts performed in good faith on behalf of the Company.

## 13. Dissolution

The Company may be dissolved upon written decision of the Member or as otherwise required by law. Upon dissolution, Company assets shall be used to pay liabilities first, then distributed to the Member.

## 14. Amendments

The Member may amend this Agreement at any time by written instrument.

## 15. Transfer of Interest

The Member may transfer all or part of the Member's interest only by written instrument. Any permitted transferee shall become a substituted member only upon written admission by the Member or the Member's legal representative.

## 16. Separation of Company and Personal Affairs

The Member shall use commercially reasonable efforts to maintain separation between Company and personal affairs, including separate banking, records, and contracts in the Company name.

## 17. Governing Law

This Agreement is governed by the laws of the State of New Jersey.

## 18. Electronic Signatures; Counterparts

This Agreement may be executed in counterparts and by electronic signature, each of which is deemed an original and all of which together constitute one instrument.
