# Boss Key LLC EIN Application Cheat Sheet

Use this on the IRS EIN tool page:
`https://www.irs.gov/businesses/small-businesses-self-employed/get-an-employer-identification-number`

## Session Rules

- Complete in one sitting (cannot save).
- Session times are Eastern Time.
- Limit is one EIN per responsible party per day.

## Answers to Use

- `Legal name of entity`: Boss Key LLC
- `Trade name / DBA`: Leave blank unless you use a separate trade name
- `Entity type`: Limited Liability Company (LLC)
- `Number of LLC members`: 1
- `State where LLC formed`: New Jersey
- `Reason for applying`: Started a new business
- `Responsible party first name`: Timothy
- `Responsible party last name`: Semenza
- `Responsible party SSN/ITIN`: [enter your number]
- `Mailing address`: 1596 Jason Dr, Cinnaminson, NJ 08077
- `Physical address`: 1596 Jason Dr, Cinnaminson, NJ 08077
- `County`: Burlington
- `Date business started or acquired`: 02/22/2026 (update if your approved NJ formation date differs)
- `Highest number of employees expected in next 12 months`: 0 (if no employees yet)
- `First date wages or annuities paid`: N/A (if no employees yet)
- `Principal activity`: Other / Consulting services
- `Principal line of business description`: Proposal management, growth strategy, and operations consulting for service businesses and public-sector contractors

## After EIN Is Issued

1. Download/print the EIN confirmation letter (CP 575 equivalent confirmation page).
2. Save it in your records folder.
3. Use EIN + formation docs to open your business bank account.
