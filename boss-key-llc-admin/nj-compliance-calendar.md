## Immediate

1. `EIN (IRS)`: Apply as soon as possible.
2. `NJ-REG (NJ tax registration)`: File within 60 days of formation and at least 15 business days before taxable activity/payroll.

## Concrete Deadlines

1. `NJ-REG latest 60-day window`: by `04/23/2026` (based on 02/22/2026 formation date).
2. `NJ Annual Report`: due each year by the last day of your anniversary month (February).
3. `2027 Annual Report target`: by `02/28/2027`.
4. `Registered Agent renewal`: around `02/22/2027` (per your service period).

## Suggested Reminder Schedule

1. `Monthly reminder`: 1st business day each month, 9:00 AM, review tax/compliance status.
2. `Annual report prep`: January 15 each year.
3. `Annual report final submit`: February 15 each year.
4. `RA renewal reminder`: January 22 each year.
