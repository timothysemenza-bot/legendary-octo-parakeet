AI RFP Digest Menu of Services

Prepared: February 25, 2026

1) NDA + Security Scoping

Outcome: legal coverage and implementation guardrails.

  • Mutual NDA execution support and scope alignment.
  • Data handling map for intake, storage, retention, and deletion.
  • Role-based access model and audit trail requirements.
  • Security constraints captured for pilot and production.

2) RFP Ingestion Pipeline

Outcome: reliable intake of all solicitation artifacts.

  • Intake from PDF, Word, email exports, and attachments.
  • OCR processing for scanned documents as needed.
  • Structured parsing into requirements, dates, and sections.
  • Version tracking across amendments and Q&A releases.

3) AI RFP Digest Engine

Outcome: decision-ready summary in hours, not days.

  • Executive summary for effort, risk, and bid posture.
  • Requirements matrix with owner and due-date mapping.
  • Risk and gap log across compliance, staffing, and technical areas.
  • Win-theme extraction and response strategy prompts.

4) Interactive Q&A Copilot

Outcome: searchable intelligence across the full corpus.

  • Natural-language Q&A over RFP package and addenda.
  • Evidence-linked answers with source references.
  • Role-tailored views for sales, legal, operations, and finance.
  • Prompt guardrails to enforce citation-backed outputs.

5) Deployment + Compliance Options

Outcome: secure architecture aligned to client policy.

  • Client-side deployment options: on-prem, private cloud, or VPC.
  • Identity and access integration planning.
  • Retention, logging, and governance control configuration.
  • Security addendum support for procurement/legal review.

6) Pilot to Production Delivery

Outcome: low-risk rollout with measurable value.

  • 2-week pilot on live or recent RFP documents.
  • Weekly checkpoint cadence with stakeholder readouts.
  • KPI tracking: time-to-digest, review effort, and risk detection.
  • Production hardening plan and ownership transition.
