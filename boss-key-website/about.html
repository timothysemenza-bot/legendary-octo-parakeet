Boss Key has two practice lines built from the same core skill: seeing pressure clearly.

I built Boss Key around pattern recognition in high-stakes environments. Today that shows up in two distinct ways: proposal management support for teams working live pursuits, and executive coaching for leaders carrying complex decisions.

The two offers

Proposal management support

For active pursuit environments that need better qualification discipline, cleaner proposal cadence, and stronger leadership visibility.

Executive coaching

For founders and operators who need a confidential thinking partner for leadership decisions, stakeholder friction, and consistent follow-through.

What changes when we work together

You hear the real issue faster

Contradictions, drag, and hidden assumptions become visible earlier.

You stop confusing activity with progress

We separate motion from actual decision quality and execution movement.

You move with cleaner intent

Teams and leaders both get clearer next actions instead of ambiguity.

Why the offerings belong under one roof

Pipeline work

In proposal support, the work lands on go/no-go decisions, proposal timing, QA gates, handoffs, and leadership escalation.

Leadership work

In coaching, the work lands on judgment, communication, stakeholder management, and how the leader carries pressure.

Who it is not for

  • Buyers who want the two offers blended into an undefined consulting bucket.
  • Teams looking only for software implementation without operating or leadership work.
  • Leaders looking for therapy rather than business coaching and advisory.
