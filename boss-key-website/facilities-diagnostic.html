What the scoring looks at

The route logic is not based on ambition. It is based on timing and control.

TimingAre you reacting to a live bid, a likely rebid, or a broad watchlist?
ControlDo you actually know how bids are qualified, staffed, priced, reviewed, and owned?
ReadinessCan the current team carry the next stage without collapsing into deadline-driven chaos?

Run the diagnostic

1. Which timing pattern is closest to your situation?
2. How strong is your incumbent and buyer knowledge?
3. How disciplined is your bid or no-bid decision process?
4. How clean is the pricing, staffing, and ops handoff?
5. How clear is compliance ownership?
6. What does your review cadence look like?
