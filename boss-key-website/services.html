Choose by what is happening right now

Option 1

Quick briefing

Right now

Best when the situation still feels broad, messy, or hard to define.

Use it for:One market, one pursuit, or one repeated growth problem.
You leave with:A clearer first move instead of a long strategy deck.
Option 2

Market watch

Before the bid

Best when your team keeps hearing about work too late and needs earlier visibility.

Use it for:Budgets, contract timing, and likely buying movement in the markets you care about.
You leave with:A sharper watchlist and better timing on where to pay attention.
Option 3

Pursuit support

Taking shape

Best when a promising target is forming and the team needs better choices, alignment, and follow-through.

Use it for:Qualification, target choice, ownership, and pursuit planning.
You leave with:A more focused pursuit and fewer late-stage surprises.
Option 4

Proposal support

Live bid

Best when the procurement is already active and the work needs cleaner structure fast.

Use it for:Reviews, compliance framing, handoffs, and delivery rhythm.
You leave with:More control inside the bid while the deadline is still manageable.
