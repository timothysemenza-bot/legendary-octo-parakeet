Optional tools, used quietly

Who this is not for

  • Organizations looking only for software implementation without leadership work.

Boundary statement: coaching vs therapy

This is executive coaching and business advisory. It is not psychotherapy, mental health treatment, or clinical care. I do not diagnose or treat mental health conditions.
