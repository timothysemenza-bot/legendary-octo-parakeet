How Boss Key works

Boss Key is designed to help clients make better growth decisions using methods they would be comfortable defending publicly.

  • Public bid portals, award notices, board packets, and capital plans
  • Company websites, public statements, job posts, and case studies
  • Client-authorized CRM history, proposal archives, and account notes
  • Clear separation of facts, inference, and uncertainty
What Boss Key does not do

The ethical boundary is part of the brand, not a legal footnote.

  • No impersonation or deceptive outreach
  • No gray-area access or credential misuse
  • No confidential-information fishing or rumor laundering
  • No lobbying and no inflated claims that public information proves more than it does
