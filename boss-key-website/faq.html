Core questions

Who do you work with?

Founder/CEOs, GMs, presidents, and operator-led leadership teams in services and operations-heavy businesses.

What problems do you help with?

Decision overload, leadership isolation, stakeholder friction, priority confusion, and inconsistent execution signals.

Is this therapy?

No. This is executive coaching and business advisory. It is not psychotherapy, mental health treatment, or diagnosis.

What does confidentiality mean here?

Our conversations are private and designed for candid leadership thinking. Any exceptions or reporting constraints are set in writing up front.

How do engagements start?

Intro call -> scope recommendation -> chosen package and cadence.

Do you use AI tools?

Only as optional execution support after clarity exists. The primary value is decision quality, not tooling.
