Frequently asked questions

Boss Key now has two separate offerings. These answers are structured the same way so it is obvious which service is for what problem.

Proposal supportExecutive coaching

Core distinction

What is the difference between proposal support and executive coaching?

Proposal support is pipeline-facing work inside live pursuits: qualification, proposal cadence, QA gates, and leadership visibility. Executive coaching is leader-facing work: judgment, stakeholder friction, communication, and decision follow-through.

Can the same client use both?

Yes, but they are scoped separately. One engagement supports the proposal environment. The other supports the leader carrying the decision load.

Proposal support questions

Who is proposal support for?

Proposal teams, BD leaders, and operators who need stronger pursuit discipline, cleaner proposal execution, and better leadership visibility.

How do proposal engagements start?

Intro conversation, then a live pursuit diagnostic sprint. If ongoing support makes sense after that, we choose the right cadence model.

What cadence options do you offer?

Diagnostic sprint for entry, then light weekly cadence support, managed pursuit operations with more recurring touchpoints, or embedded support for higher-volume environments.

Do you replace my internal proposal lead?

No. The work is meant to strengthen decision cadence, quality control, and cross-functional follow-through around the team you already have.

Are you selling software or an AI product?

No. The offer is operating support and execution cadence around live pursuits, not software access.

Executive coaching questions

Who is coaching for?

Founder/CEOs, GMs, presidents, and operator-led leaders who need a confidential thought partner for difficult decisions.

How does coaching usually run?

Weekly or biweekly for sustained support, short advisory sprints for one concentrated decision cycle, or a lighter retainer for ongoing coverage.

Is coaching therapy?

No. This is executive coaching and business advisory. It is not psychotherapy, mental health treatment, or diagnosis.

What does confidentiality mean here?

The work is designed as a private leadership space for candid thinking. Any exceptions or reporting constraints are set in writing up front.

Commercial and fit questions

How is pricing structured?

Pricing depends on cadence, decision complexity, and support depth. Final scope and fees are confirmed after the intro conversation.

Who is not a fit?

Teams looking only for tool implementation, buyers who want proposal support and coaching blended into a vague bucket, and anyone seeking clinical mental health care.

Still deciding?

If the real problem is inside the pipeline, start with proposal support. If the real problem is inside the leader's decision load, start with coaching.
