Frequently asked questions

Boss Key is structured around procurement intelligence, capture strategy, and proposal execution. These answers explain where the work begins, what it includes, and what it is not.

Core model

What is Boss Key?

A government opportunity intelligence and capture advisory firm that helps clients identify, interpret, and act on public-sector signals before formal procurements are released.

Are you a lobbying firm?

No. Boss Key does not provide lobbying services. The work is grounded in public information, policy interpretation, funding movement, procurement signals, and ethical capture advisory.

Do you still support proposals?

Yes. Proposal execution remains part of the model, but it is the downstream expansion service, not the lead identity.

Who the firm is for

Who is the best fit?

Mid-size contractors, service providers, nonprofits, and selected public-sector vendors that want earlier visibility into state and local opportunities and are willing to act before the bid is posted.

What sectors are most aligned?

Facilities and municipal services first, then healthcare and human services, education support, nonprofits pursuing government funding, and technology vendors entering regional public-sector markets.

What if we only want post-release bid writing?

Boss Key is probably not the best fit if you want only generic bid writing with no interest in upstream opportunity timing or capture strategy.

How work starts

What is the easiest way to start?

Start with an opportunity briefing, then use the Opportunity Foresight Sprint as the fastest paid entry point if you want a defined test of fit.

What does the sprint include?

A target-market signal scan, likely opportunity forecast, top-priority pursuit shortlist, capture briefs for the strongest targets, and a partner recommendation call.

What happens after the sprint?

Clients usually move into a procurement intelligence retainer, an opportunity-specific capture strategy engagement, or proposal execution support when the procurement becomes formal.

Commercial structure

How do you price engagements?

Boss Key is designed around recurring retainers plus fixed-fee strategy and proposal projects. Pricing is tied to research scope, signal complexity, and advisory cadence.

Do you work on contingency?

Only selectively and in narrow cases. The default model is retainer plus project work, because the value is the judgment and operating discipline applied before an award exists.

What are the boundaries?

No lobbying, no improper procurement influence, no indefinite channel commissions, and no software-led engagement disguised as consulting.

Still deciding?

If your problem is lack of early market visibility, start with intelligence. If you already have a likely target and need pre-RFP positioning, start with capture strategy. If the bid is live, start with proposal execution support.
