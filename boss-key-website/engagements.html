Ways to work together

Entry Sprint

Live Pursuit Diagnostic Sprint (30 days)

Best for: Teams that need immediate clarity on 1-2 live pursuits before choosing a longer support model.

Cadence: Weekly decision checkpoints plus applied support across live opportunities.

You get: Baseline snapshot, weekly decision memos, bottleneck map, and next-phase recommendation.

Light Retainer

Pursuit Cadence Support

Best for: Teams needing decision discipline without daily embedded support.

Cadence: 1 weekly operating review plus async decision support.

You get: Go/no-go support, milestone QA, and clearer handoffs on active pursuits.

Standard Retainer

Managed Pursuit Operations

Best for: Teams running multiple active pursuits each month and needing stronger throughput control.

Cadence: 2 weekly operating blocks, pursuit-specific checkpoints, and a weekly leadership summary.

You get: Recurring operating support, active proposal coverage, QA discipline, and leadership visibility.

Embedded Support

Embedded Capture Partner

Best for: High-volume or high-stakes environments where close day-to-day support is required.

Cadence: Multi-touch weekly engagement plus a leadership escalation channel.

You get: Embedded pursuit leadership, cross-functional coordination, QA gates, and reduced late-cycle churn.

Cadence, frequency, and operating rhythm

Weekly pursuit checkpoint

Structured review of active pursuits to confirm priority, unblock work, assign owners, and set next decisions.

Go/no-go review rhythm

Standing decision review to evaluate fit, resource load, and win path before drafting capacity gets committed.

Proposal readiness QA gates

Predefined quality gates at key milestones so teams catch compliance and execution risk before submission week.

Leadership visibility loop

Weekly decision memo covering status, risks, escalations, and required executive actions.

What gets measured

Decision cycle time

How quickly opportunities move from intake to a real go/no-go decision.

Time to first compliant draft

Whether proposal production is getting faster without increasing chaos.

Leadership decision latency

How long escalations sit before someone resolves them clearly.

What this is not

  • No software sale or implementation-led engagement.
  • No documentation-only scope detached from live pursuits.
  • No daily task execution substitute for internal ownership unless explicitly scoped as embedded support.

How engagements start

Intro conversation -> live pursuit diagnostic -> retainer recommendation if ongoing support makes sense. That keeps the work grounded in your actual pursuit environment instead of guessing from the outside.
