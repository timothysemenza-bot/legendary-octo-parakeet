Packages

Option A

Executive Coaching (weekly or biweekly)

Who it fits: Founders and operators with sustained decision pressure.

Included: 1:1 sessions, decision calibration, between-session accountability notes.

Cadence: Weekly or biweekly.

Typical duration: 3-6 months.

Pricing: Shared after intro based on scope, cadence, and support depth.

Option B

Executive Advisory Sprint (2-4 weeks)

Who it fits: Leaders facing one critical decision cycle right now.

Included: Decision map, stakeholder pressure-test, options memo, action sequence.

Cadence: 1-2 sessions per week plus focused async support.

Typical duration: 2-4 weeks.

Pricing: Shared after intro based on scope, cadence, and support depth.

Option C

Ongoing Advisory Retainer (lightweight)

Who it fits: Leaders who want continuing confidential advisory coverage.

Included: Monthly calls, async check-ins, decision support on active leadership issues.

Cadence: Monthly call + async channel.

Typical duration: 6+ months.

Pricing: Shared after intro based on scope, cadence, and support depth.

All packages are business coaching and advisory engagements. They do not provide psychotherapy, mental health treatment, or medical services.
