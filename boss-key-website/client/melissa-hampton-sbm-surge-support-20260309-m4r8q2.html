SBM Pursuit Surge Support

On-demand proposal and pursuit support for quick-turn, high-priority growth work when the internal team is already carrying too many active priorities.

Prepared for: Melissa Hampton / SBM internal discussion Date: March 9, 2026 Use case: Overflow, quick-turn, and backfill support

Business Need

From the call, the gap is clear: SBM is managing a high volume of active pursuits while also protecting internal focus on the highest-priority growth RFPs and deals. When a large proposal turns quickly, the team can run out of clean bandwidth even when the internal structure is sound.

What This Is

  • External surge capacity for proposal and pursuit execution.
  • A way to protect internal focus during compressed timelines.
  • Support that plugs into the existing sales, pricing, and operations structure.

Where This Fits

Best-Fit Scenarios

  • Large proposals with a 4-day or similarly compressed turnaround.
  • Simultaneous live pursuits that overload the internal team.
  • High-priority growth work that should not be delayed by lower-value noise.
  • Temporary bandwidth gaps or backfill situations.

What Stays Internal

  • Final pricing ownership and pricing-team decisions.
  • Client relationship ownership and executive approvals.
  • Final go/no-go and leadership sign-off.
  • Any confidential materials not cleared for external handling.

Support Model

Rapid Response

  • Jump in fast when a pursuit compresses unexpectedly.
  • Stabilize scope, timeline, and document flow.

Overflow Delivery

  • Own specific proposal workstreams under internal direction.
  • Reduce load on the core team during peak volume.

Strategic Backfill

  • Cover temporary gaps while the team scales or rebalances.
  • Support continuity without changing internal ownership lines.

What Boss Key Would Handle

  • Proposal management and submission-path coordination.
  • Draft development, structure, and response shaping.
  • Compliance and content assembly support.
  • Executive summary and storyline refinement.
  • Cross-functional coordination with sales, pricing, and operations inputs.
  • Fast-turn document cleanup and response acceleration.
  • Additional perspective on gaps, risks, and missing proof points.
  • Flexible support aligned to the way SBM already runs deals.

Why This Helps SBM

Protects Core Team Focus

Keeps the team centered on the most important growth work instead of spreading thin across every urgent request.

Absorbs Peak Load

Creates a cleaner way to handle overflow when proposal volume spikes or timelines collapse.

Maintains Existing Structure

Supports the current decision model rather than forcing a new process or a new layer of ownership.

If aligned: hold a short follow-up conversation to define trigger conditions, confidentiality boundaries, and what work can be safely handled externally.
