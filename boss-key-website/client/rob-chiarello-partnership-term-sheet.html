Responsibilities: Software Partner

  • Provide platform reliability, onboarding support, and user training resources.

Due Diligence Gates (Required Before White Label)

Product Gate
Pilot scenarios must prove acceptable output quality, formatting readiness, and reviewer usability in realistic US workflows.
Security Gate
Software Partner provides written details on encryption, sub-processors, retention/deletion controls, incident response, and tenant separation.
Legal Gate
Mutual agreement on MSA terms including liability, confidentiality, IP boundaries, and termination rights.
Service Gate
Defined support ownership, SLA expectations, and escalation paths for client-facing issues.

Core Terms

Client Ownership
Each party retains ownership of its own direct client relationships unless otherwise documented in a client-specific SOW.
Brand Usage
No logo, name, or marketing claims may be used without prior written approval from the owning party.
Data Access
Boss Key client data may only be used for contracted service delivery and never for unrelated model training or external disclosure.
Term + Exit
30-day pilot window. Either party may terminate pilot collaboration with written notice; confidentiality obligations survive.
