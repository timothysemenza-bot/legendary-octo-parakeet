Pilot Scope

Transcript-to-CRM Compliance Automation

A focused 2-week pilot to convert discovery call transcripts into structured CRM updates and next-action tasks, so sales activity gets captured without relying on rep admin discipline.

Core Outcome

Increase CRM completion and manager visibility while reducing admin drag on your sales team.

Approve Pilot Book Kickoff

Pilot Snapshot

2 Weeks

Client: Broadspire Sales Team

Format: transcript ingest, field mapping, CRM-ready updates, manager digest, QA log

Problem This Solves

  1. Reps complete calls but do not consistently update CRM records.
  2. Sales leadership lacks reliable activity visibility for coaching and forecasting.
  3. Manual follow-up logging creates avoidable overhead and delay.

In Scope

  1. Ingest transcript text from discovery calls.
  2. Map pain points, buying signals, objections, and agreed next step.
  3. Generate CRM-ready updates plus follow-up tasks.
  4. Generate manager summary email or daily digest.
  5. Maintain auditable QA log for refinement.

Out Of Scope (Pilot)

  1. Full CRM migration.
  2. End-to-end outbound sequencing build.
  3. Legal/compliance policy redesign.
  4. Cross-department workflow redesign beyond sales capture.

Required Inputs

  1. 10 to 20+ sample call transcripts.
  2. CRM schema and required field list.
  3. One sales-manager reviewer for quality checks.
  4. Handoff preference: API push or email/drop-file import.

Deliverables

  1. Pilot workflow map (input → parse → update → review).
  2. Transcript-to-CRM mapping template.
  3. Manager summary format (single-call and daily digest).
  4. QA log with detected gaps and tuning notes.
  5. End-of-pilot recommendation: scale, revise, or stop.

Success Metrics

CRM completion rate Target: +40% or better vs baseline.
Call-to-update speed Target: under 15 minutes after call end.
Rep admin burden Target: 30%+ reduction in manual update time.
Manager confidence Target: quality score 1-5 tracked across pilot calls.

Implementation Plan

Week 1

  1. Confirm required schema and update fields.
  2. Build initial extraction and mapping logic.
  3. Run historical transcript QA and adjust rules.

Week 2

  1. Process live calls daily.
  2. Tune extraction from manager feedback.
  3. Finalize readout and rollout recommendation.

Commercial Frame

  1. Pilot option: fixed fee at $5,500.
  2. Retainer path: apply pilot fee credit toward $6,000/month for 3-month minimum support.
Decision checkpoint at end of week 2: scale to full team, continue manager-only workflow, or pause with lessons learned.
