Boss Key Pursuit Operating System

An AI-assisted pursuit and proposal workflow that helps founder-led teams qualify faster, respond faster, and reduce manual BD/admin load.

Prepared For

Ann Highsmith, Talos (Intro/Pilot Discussion)

  • Primary Goal: Reduce proposal and lead qualification overhead while keeping execution quality high.
  • Pilot Intent: Use Talos as a live partner account to tune workflows and prove measurable speed gains.
  • Buying Motion: Board-ready pilot packet with clear one-time and recurring options.

Current Pain Pattern

  • Founder/exec is doing site walks, proposal writing, qualification, and pricing checks personally.
  • Government and enterprise submissions are long, inconsistent, and hard to score quickly.
  • Lead-gen channels produce noise; quality intros and pre-qualified targets are scarce.
  • Growth stalls when BD output depends on one person.

Core Capabilities

1) Qualification Engine

Scores opportunities against ICP, deal size, fit criteria, and likely win path.

2) Go/No-Go Support

Flags low-probability pursuits early so effort stays on winnable work.

3) Proposal Draft Acceleration

Transforms source data into first-pass response packages and checklists.

4) Multi-Profile Targeting

Supports different market profiles by geography, vertical, and account type.

5) Pre-Meeting Intelligence

Builds concise prep notes before calls so outreach becomes more relevant.

6) Handoff-Ready Output

Packages key facts, assumptions, and actions so teams can execute with less rework.

Operating Flow

  1. Ingest opportunity context (RFP, account notes, target profile).
  2. Run fit and probability checks.
  3. Generate pursuit recommendation + draft content package.
  4. Review and finalize for submission or outreach.
  5. Track outcomes and improve scoring logic each cycle.

Pilot Deliverables

  • Configured Talos pursuit profile (ICP + qualification logic).
  • Pilot playbook with workflow, roles, and QA checkpoints.
  • Board-facing summary format for pilot updates.
  • One-page vertical expansion sheet (included as Page 2).

Target outcome: move from multi-week admin-heavy cycles to faster, repeatable qualification and first-draft output with tighter focus on high-value accounts.
