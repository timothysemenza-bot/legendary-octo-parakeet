Translate public-sector signals into earlier opportunity visibility.

What Boss Key monitors

Funding and budget signals

Proposed and adopted budgets, capital plans, appropriations, bond programs, hearings, and pass-through funding movement.

Legislative and regulatory signals

Bills, committee activity, rulemaking, executive action, licensing changes, and program-specific policy movement.

Procurement-adjacent signals

Contract expirations, rebid windows, amendments, board approvals, vendor outreach, and procurement forecasts.

What this is not

Not lobbying

Boss Key does not provide lobbying services. The work is based on public information, ethical analysis, and structured capture planning.

Not passive monitoring

The goal is not to send a stream of alerts. The goal is to translate signals into opportunity hypotheses and concrete pre-RFP actions.

Best fit

  • Organizations with public-sector growth goals but weak pre-RFP visibility
  • Mid-size contractors and service providers expanding in state and local markets
  • Nonprofits and public-sector entrants that need help connecting policy and funding movement to actual opportunity timing
