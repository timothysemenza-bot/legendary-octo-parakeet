Book intro

Proof across both Boss Key offerings.

Proposal support and executive coaching solve different problems, so the proof should be split the same way. The throughline is better decision quality under pressure and cleaner follow-through after the decision is made.

View proposal supportView coaching

Proposal support outcomes

Situation: Active pipeline had too many borderline pursuits moving forward. Friction: Drafting capacity was getting committed before real qualification decisions were made. Support: Introduced clearer go/no-go cadence and decision ownership. Observed shift: Earlier screening and cleaner use of proposal resources.

Situation: Proposal deadlines kept compressing into the final days. Friction: Teams were discovering gaps late and escalating under avoidable pressure. Support: Added milestone QA gates and recurring pursuit checkpoints. Observed shift: Better visibility earlier in the cycle and fewer late surprises.

Situation: Leadership could not tell which pursuits were truly at risk. Friction: Escalations were inconsistent and decisions lagged. Support: Weekly decision memos and explicit escalation points. Observed shift: Faster leadership response and clearer operating priorities.

Executive coaching outcomes

Situation: Founder balancing conflicting growth priorities. Friction: Team received mixed signals and momentum slowed. Support: Decision calibration and priority sequencing. Observed shift: Clearer direction and steadier execution rhythm.

Situation: Business unit leader under high external pressure. Friction: Reactive decision swings and short-term noise. Support: Structured decision review and risk framing. Observed shift: More stable choices and stronger leadership consistency.

Situation: Cross-functional transition with political tension. Friction: Escalations and unclear ownership. Support: Role-clarity alignment and decision sequencing support. Observed shift: Cleaner handoffs and fewer avoidable escalations.

What tends to improve

Decision speed

Teams and leaders both spend less time circling obvious calls.

Signal quality

Risks, tradeoffs, and ownership become more visible earlier.

Execution follow-through

Decisions translate into cleaner action instead of drift.

What this proof is not claiming

  • No fabricated metrics or inflated “transformation” stories.
  • No attempt to collapse proposal work and coaching into one vague consulting claim.
  • No promise that process alone or insight alone fixes every execution issue.

Next step

If the pressure is inside your live pipeline, start with proposal support. If the pressure is inside your leadership decisions, start with coaching.
