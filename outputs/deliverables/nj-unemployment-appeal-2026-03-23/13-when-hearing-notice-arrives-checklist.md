# When The Hearing Notice Arrives

Prepared: March 23, 2026

Appeal reference number: `00712347`

Use this as a quick checklist when the NJ Appeal Tribunal notice shows up.

## Same day the notice arrives

- Read the notice all the way through once before doing anything else.
- Write down the hearing date, hearing time, registration instructions, and any deadline for sending documents.
- Save a PDF or photo of the notice.
- Put the hearing date on your calendar with two reminders.
- If the notice gives a registration number or call-in instruction, register as soon as possible.
- Save the hearing registration confirmation number if one is given.

## Information to keep together

- your appeal reference number: `00712347`
- the hearing notice itself
- any docket number listed on the hearing notice
- the phone number where you want the Appeals Examiner to reach you
- a backup phone number if you have one

## Documents to gather right away

- Notice of Determination
- the Gmail `Severance Agreement` thread
- the signed-and-returned separation agreement copy in your possession
- the March 13, 2026 pay statement showing severance
- the February 19, 2026 clarification screenshots
- the April 30, 2025 journal entry
- the February 10, 2026 Tracey call transcript and analysis

## Before sending any exhibits

- check the hearing notice for exact instructions
- follow the notice if it tells you where and when to send documents
- use the same exhibit set you actually plan to rely on
- number your exhibits clearly
- avoid sending extra material that does not help prove the separation issue

## One short prep task to do immediately

- write a one-page chronology with:
- when the work-related problem began
- who was involved
- what you reported internally
- what the employer did or failed to do
- why continuing to work was no longer reasonable
- how the severance process unfolded

## One week before the hearing

- read your hearing statement out loud once or twice
- review your likely questions and answers
- make a clean copy of your exhibits for yourself
- highlight dates, names, and key phrases on your personal copy only
- decide what order you want to tell the story in

## Day before the hearing

- confirm the hearing time again
- confirm the phone number NJ should call
- turn off call blocking, spam screening, and silence-unknown-caller features
- charge your phone fully
- pick a quiet place where you will not be interrupted
- keep water, your notes, and your exhibit set in front of you

## Day of the hearing

- be ready early
- answer calls from private, unknown, or out-of-area numbers
- keep your answers short first
- do not guess if you do not know a date
- do not deny resignation language exists in the paperwork or Tracey call
- explain the full context, including the severance and offboarding process

## If something is wrong with the notice

- if the date or time will not work, contact the Appeal Tribunal immediately
- if the phone number on the notice is wrong, correct it immediately
- if the notice mentions exhibits and you have not sent them yet, do that right away

## Keep doing this while waiting

- continue certifying weekly for benefits
- keep every new appeal letter, email, and envelope
- save any newly found evidence in this folder
