# First Light Prompt Pack

## Use This Pack
This document is model-agnostic. Reuse the same continuity anchors and negative prompts across every shot so the memory world stays coherent. If a model handles long prompts poorly, keep the global continuity block intact and shorten the shot-specific descriptive language first.

## Global Continuity Anchors
Paste or adapt these anchors into every prompt:

- Timeless early-morning family home with wood floors, sheer curtains, soft pale walls, simple trim, modest sink, and leafy yard beyond the window.
- Small child, age 5 to 7, soft brown hair, off-white cotton sleepwear, bare feet, face mostly hidden or turned away.
- Adult presence appears only in fragments: hands, shoulder, passing silhouette, or reflection.
- Warm gold, pale green, and soft blue palette with low-contrast shadows.
- Natural window light, airborne dust, reflective water, soft shallow focus, floating camera, intimate memory tone.
- Grounded realism with gentle impressionism, not fantasy, not horror, not commercial gloss.

## Global Negative Prompts
- no text, captions, subtitles, watermark, logo, brand name
- no modern electronics, phone, tablet, television, laptop, plastic toy clutter
- no hard sci-fi, neon lighting, cyberpunk color, surreal creature, dream monster
- no glossy ad look, fashion editorial styling, luxury showroom interior
- no uncanny smile, staring directly into camera, exaggerated emotion, melodramatic acting
- no duplicated limbs, malformed hands, extra fingers, warped architecture
- no extreme lens distortion, oversharpening, crunchy HDR, artificial bokeh overlays

## Shot Plan

### Shot 1 - Curtains And Dust
**Duration:** 6 seconds
**Story purpose:** Open inside sensation before language.
**Voiceover fragment:** "Before I knew the names for anything,"
**Subject:** Dawn light moving through sheer curtains near a child's bed.
**Environment:** Bedroom corner in a modest family home.
**Time of day:** Very early morning.
**Lighting behavior:** Soft sunrise backlight; dust visible in the beam.
**Lens and composition:** Medium-wide frame, child only as soft blanket silhouette or shoulder shape.
**Camera motion:** Slow drift inward, almost breathing with the curtain.
**Emotional intent:** First awareness, hushed wonder.
**Continuity anchors:** Keep the child indistinct; the light is the main character.
**Prompt:** Timeless early-morning bedroom, sheer curtains breathing in a faint breeze, warm sunrise light pouring through the window and turning dust into visible particles, small child under light cotton blanket seen only as a soft silhouette near frame edge, pale walls, old wood trim, intimate grounded realism, shallow focus, floating camera drifting slowly inward, low contrast, tactile fabric, reverent memory tone.
**Negative prompt:** Use the global negative prompts. Also avoid sharp facial detail, busy decor, toys, posters, and dramatic shadow patterns.
**Fallback still/parallax version:** Same composition, with the curtain and dust as the dominant shapes; hold for a subtle 2.5D push-in if video motion fails.

### Shot 2 - Floorboards
**Duration:** 6 seconds
**Story purpose:** Move memory into the body.
**Voiceover fragment:** "I knew the light."
**Subject:** Bare feet meeting striped morning light on floorboards.
**Environment:** Same home, beside bed or near doorway.
**Time of day:** Early morning.
**Lighting behavior:** Window light creates long, soft bands across the floor.
**Lens and composition:** Low close shot at ankle height.
**Camera motion:** Gentle lateral drift.
**Emotional intent:** Discovery through touch.
**Continuity anchors:** Same off-white sleepwear; child face unseen.
**Prompt:** Low intimate close shot of a small child stepping barefoot onto old wood floorboards crossed by soft morning light, off-white cotton sleepwear brushing the calves, dust and warmth in the air, naturalistic family home interior, shallow depth of field, floating side drift, tender tactile realism, light catching skin and wood grain.
**Negative prompt:** Use the global negative prompts. Also avoid polished modern floors, slippers, socks, and perfectly symmetrical framing.
**Fallback still/parallax version:** Freeze on the first step into light with strong wood texture and a gentle digital drift from left to right.

### Shot 3 - Hand In The Beam
**Duration:** 7 seconds
**Story purpose:** Make wonder active and childlike.
**Voiceover fragment:** "It came through the curtains and turned dust into weather."
**Subject:** Small hand moving slowly through a beam of dusty light.
**Environment:** Same bedroom or hallway edge.
**Time of day:** Early morning.
**Lighting behavior:** Thick visible particulate in a strong but soft beam.
**Lens and composition:** Close-up of hand and forearm; face absent.
**Camera motion:** Slow push-in following the hand.
**Emotional intent:** Curiosity and awe.
**Continuity anchors:** Same child, same light system, same home.
**Prompt:** Close-up of a young child's hand moving slowly through a bright shaft of early-morning window light, dust shimmering around the fingers like tiny weather, off-white sleeve, warm gold highlights, pale green and blue undertones in shadow, soft-focus falloff, intimate tactile realism, floating camera with a slow push-in, quiet memory tone, no face visible.
**Negative prompt:** Use the global negative prompts. Also avoid glitter effects, magical sparkles, supernatural glow, and stylized particle graphics.
**Fallback still/parallax version:** Hold on the hand suspended in the beam with dust layered in foreground and background for a deep parallax move.

### Shot 4 - Bowl In The Sink
**Duration:** 6 seconds
**Story purpose:** Introduce the domestic heartbeat of the house.
**Voiceover fragment:** "It found the floorboards, my hands, the bowl in the sink,"
**Subject:** Sunlight trembling across water in a ceramic bowl while an adult hand briefly enters frame.
**Environment:** Simple kitchen sink area in the same home.
**Time of day:** Early morning.
**Lighting behavior:** Window reflection dances across water and ceramic.
**Lens and composition:** Tight close shot on bowl, sink edge, and hand fragment.
**Camera motion:** Nearly still with a subtle observational drift.
**Emotional intent:** Ordinary life becoming sacred.
**Continuity anchors:** Adult present only as hand or wrist; keep home modest.
**Prompt:** Tight close shot of a simple ceramic bowl sitting in a kitchen sink, early-morning window light trembling across shallow water, an adult hand briefly entering frame to steady the bowl, modest family kitchen, soft neutral tones, natural texture, reflective highlights, intimate realism, low contrast, shallow focus, almost-still camera with a soft drift.
**Negative prompt:** Use the global negative prompts. Also avoid stainless steel showroom kitchens, luxury counters, strong specular glare, and visible faces.
**Fallback still/parallax version:** Use a still focused on water reflections and ceramic texture; animate a gentle light shimmer in post if needed.

### Shot 5 - Leaves At The Window
**Duration:** 6 seconds
**Story purpose:** Open the house toward the outside world.
**Voiceover fragment:** "the leaves outside the window."
**Subject:** Leaves moving beyond the glass, with a faint child reflection.
**Environment:** Window inside the same home facing a leafy yard.
**Time of day:** Early morning.
**Lighting behavior:** Interior glow meets cool exterior greens.
**Lens and composition:** Medium close on glass plane; exterior softly dominant.
**Camera motion:** Slow tilt or drift that catches reflection.
**Emotional intent:** Threshold between inside and outside.
**Continuity anchors:** Reflection should be partial and soft, not a clean face reveal.
**Prompt:** Quiet morning window inside a modest family home, soft green leaves moving outside in gentle wind, faint partial reflection of a small child in the glass, warm interior light meeting cool outdoor greens, sheer curtain edge at frame, shallow focus, floating camera drift, grounded lyrical realism, reflective surfaces handled gently, intimate memory atmosphere.
**Negative prompt:** Use the global negative prompts. Also avoid storm weather, dramatic rain, perfect mirror reflection, and a sharp centered portrait in glass.
**Fallback still/parallax version:** Build a layered still with glass, curtain, reflection, and leaves on separate planes for a slow push and slight rack-focus effect.

### Shot 6 - Passing Presence
**Duration:** 7 seconds
**Story purpose:** Let the adult presence shape the memory without taking it over.
**Voiceover fragment:** "Someone moved in the next room, humming,"
**Subject:** A softly backlit adult silhouette crossing a hallway or doorway.
**Environment:** Hallway connected to bedroom and kitchen spaces in the same home.
**Time of day:** Early morning.
**Lighting behavior:** Warm doorway glow with softer shadow in the hall.
**Lens and composition:** Medium shot with child foreground blur or doorframe occlusion.
**Camera motion:** Soft pan or drift following the passing motion.
**Emotional intent:** Safety, distance, and everyday grace.
**Continuity anchors:** Adult face should remain hidden; movement should feel ordinary.
**Prompt:** Timeless domestic hallway in early morning, warm doorway light spilling into a quieter hall, a parent-like adult figure crossing frame as a soft backlit silhouette, face not visible, child presence suggested as a blurred shoulder or foreground edge, modest home textures, shallow depth of field, floating camera with a delicate pan, grounded realism, intimate memory tone, protective and ordinary rather than dramatic.
**Negative prompt:** Use the global negative prompts. Also avoid horror framing, ominous posture, sharp noir shadows, and direct eye contact.
**Fallback still/parallax version:** Hold on the doorway with the adult caught mid-passage in soft motion blur; simulate drift with a pan across the hallway planes.

### Shot 7 - Outside Touch
**Duration:** 6 seconds
**Story purpose:** Extend the memory into the living world beyond the house.
**Voiceover fragment:** "and I learned the house was alive"
**Subject:** Child fingertips brushing dew-heavy grass or leaves just outside.
**Environment:** Yard or garden immediately outside the home.
**Time of day:** Early morning.
**Lighting behavior:** Low sun grazing grass and skin.
**Lens and composition:** Tight close shot on hand and vegetation.
**Camera motion:** Slow tracking motion parallel to the hand.
**Emotional intent:** The house and world breathing together.
**Continuity anchors:** Keep wardrobe consistent; keep the scale childlike.
**Prompt:** Close tactile shot of a small child's fingertips brushing through dew-touched grass or soft leaves just outside a family home at sunrise, off-white cotton sleepwear visible at edge of frame, warm gold light grazing skin and green textures, shallow focus, floating tracking motion, grounded natural realism, intimate memory atmosphere, soft breeze, no face visible.
**Negative prompt:** Use the global negative prompts. Also avoid bright midday sun, garden perfection, insects in macro detail, and fantasy sparkle.
**Fallback still/parallax version:** Use a still of hand and grass with layered dew highlights and a slight horizontal drift.

### Shot 8 - Water And Sky Insert
**Duration:** 6 seconds
**Story purpose:** Briefly widen the memory toward something larger than the room.
**Voiceover fragment:** "before I learned the word for home."
**Subject:** Sunlit water surface that almost resembles sky or stars through bokeh.
**Environment:** Reflective outdoor water or abstracted basin surface that still feels real.
**Time of day:** Morning.
**Lighting behavior:** Bright reflected sun softened by lens bloom and shallow focus.
**Lens and composition:** Impressionistic close shot, no people visible.
**Camera motion:** Slow hover with tiny ripple response.
**Emotional intent:** Expansion, mystery, scale.
**Continuity anchors:** Keep it grounded in real light and water; do not go fully cosmic.
**Prompt:** Impressionistic close view of morning light glancing across a rippling water surface, highlights breaking into soft star-like bokeh while remaining clearly real water, pale blue and warm gold tones, shallow focus, delicate lens bloom, floating camera hovering just above the surface, grounded poetic realism, brief sense of vastness without becoming abstract fantasy.
**Negative prompt:** Use the global negative prompts. Also avoid literal galaxies, outer space imagery, heavy VFX particles, and psychedelic color shifts.
**Fallback still/parallax version:** Use a still water texture with animated ripple displacement and subtle push-in.

### Shot 9 - Adult Return
**Duration:** 8 seconds
**Story purpose:** Return to present-day awareness and land the thesis.
**Voiceover fragment:** "I think that is where memory begins: not in language, but in glow, water, breath, and the feeling of being held inside the morning."
**Subject:** Adult hand resting on a windowsill in soft morning light, echoing the child hand earlier.
**Environment:** Present-day room that still feels connected to the remembered house.
**Time of day:** Morning.
**Lighting behavior:** Gentle window bloom over older skin and dust in air.
**Lens and composition:** Medium close on hand, sill, curtain edge, and partial torso or shoulder.
**Camera motion:** Slow inward drift, then hold.
**Emotional intent:** Recognition, gratitude, and calm.
**Continuity anchors:** Adult remains mostly fragmentary; keep the same palette and domestic realism.
**Prompt:** Present-day adult standing beside a morning window in a quiet room connected in feeling to the remembered house, one hand resting lightly on the sill as dust turns visible in soft light, sheer curtain edge moving, warm gold and pale blue palette, shallow focus, floating slow push-in that settles into stillness, intimate grounded realism, calm recognition, no full face reveal, visual echo of the child's earlier hand in light.
**Negative prompt:** Use the global negative prompts. Also avoid obvious age makeup, overt sadness, dramatic crying, and a clean modern apartment look.
**Fallback still/parallax version:** Hold on the hand, curtain, and window light; add only the smallest drift and curtain movement in post.

## Fallback Assembly Rule
If full motion clips fail for one or more shots, preserve the structure in this order:

1. Keep video for Shots 1, 3, and 9 if possible. They carry the emotional identity.
2. Convert Shots 4, 5, 7, and 8 to stills with parallax or light animation first.
3. If character consistency breaks, remove or soften all shots that imply a full face before removing environmental shots.
4. Maintain the same voiceover timing even if picture becomes more photographic than cinematic.

## Prompting Notes
- Do not mention specific filmmakers in the prompts.
- Keep motion simple. Most clips should feel like one clean camera gesture.
- Generate multiple variants for Shots 1, 3, 6, and 9 before moving on; they define the film's tone.
- Reject outputs that look too polished, too literal, or too emotionally performative.
