UConn Writing Center Alumni Tribute Pilot

A tribute-first phase 1 pilot for Tom Deans that uses Margie Ouimette's March 31, 2026 retirement reception to test lightweight alumni outreach, collect reply-ready memories and well-wishes, and stand up a reusable workflow for later alumni reactivation without assuming a standalone custom site build.

Prepared for: Tom Deans, University of Connecticut Writing Center Date: March 16, 2026 Visible sender: Tom via UConn email Response deadline: March 27, 2026 at 5:00 PM ET

Phase 1 Objective

Collect and curate alumni memories and well-wishes for Margie while building a reply-based, approval-gated outreach process that can later support a broader alumni network, reporting, and development work without forcing those later-phase goals into the first live touchpoint. If helpful, that process can include a lightweight public intake path on the existing Writing Center site or another approved channel, but the workflow matters more than a custom build.

Why This First

Immediate, human reason to reconnect

Margie's March 31 reception creates a natural, generous reason for former tutors to reply. The ask is meaningful on its own and does not read like fundraising or program marketing.

Low-friction test of the larger system

A tribute campaign lets the Writing Center validate list-building, message tone, permissions handling, and curation workflow before attempting broader alumni reactivation.
