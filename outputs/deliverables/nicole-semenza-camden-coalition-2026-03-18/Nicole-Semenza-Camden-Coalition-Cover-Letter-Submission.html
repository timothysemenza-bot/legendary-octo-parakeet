Dear Hiring Committee,

I am excited to apply for the Program Manager, Policy and Advocacy position with the Camden Coalition. Your mission to improve care and outcomes for people with complex health and social needs resonates deeply with me, and the opportunity to support policy and advocacy efforts grounded in healthcare delivery reform, housing, transportation, and behavioral health is especially compelling.

Over the past eight years, I have built a career at the intersection of policy research, public affairs, and coalition engagement. At Venture Global Consulting, I advanced client policy priorities through direct engagement with legislators, legislative staff, and external stakeholders, while developing advocacy materials including op-eds, letters of support, and reports. At CADCA, I managed bipartisan stakeholder engagement across congressional chambers and developed advocacy toolkits and legislative alerts used by more than 5,000 coalition members. Earlier, as a Legislative Fellow with the Women's Congressional Policy Institute, I managed a congressional health policy portfolio and coordinated stakeholder engagement to inform legislative priorities.

I would also bring strong health policy research and writing experience to this role. At the Prenatal-to-Three Policy Impact Center at the LBJ School of Public Affairs, I conducted evidence reviews and policy research on issues including Medicaid expansion, group prenatal care, doula services, and screening programs for children. That work required synthesizing complex information into technical briefs, reports, and policy-facing materials for agencies and decision-makers. Across these roles, I have consistently supported cross-functional work that depends on careful coordination, clear written communication, relationship-building, and strong follow-through.

What draws me to this opportunity is the chance to contribute those skills in service of vulnerable populations in my home region. As a South Jersey-based policy professional, I value the Camden Coalition's commitment to practical systems change shaped by partnership with providers, public agencies, and communities. I am excited by the opportunity to help convene partners, support coalition activities, develop policy communications, track project progress, and contribute to advocacy efforts that improve health and social outcomes. After spending the last several years focused on full-time caregiving, I am eager to return to full-time mission-driven work in a role where thoughtful program management and policy engagement can make a tangible difference.

Thank you for your consideration. I would welcome the opportunity to discuss how my background in health policy, advocacy communications, and stakeholder coordination could support the Camden Coalition's policy and advocacy work.

Sincerely,
