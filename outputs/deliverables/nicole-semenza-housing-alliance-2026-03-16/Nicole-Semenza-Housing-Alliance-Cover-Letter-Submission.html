Dear Hiring Committee,

I am excited to apply for the Policy Director position with the Housing Alliance of Pennsylvania. Safe, decent, and affordable housing is foundational to health, family stability, and opportunity, which is why your mission resonates so strongly with me. I am especially drawn to the Housing Alliance's coalition model and commitment to elevating provider and lived-experience perspectives in state and federal advocacy.

Over the past eight years, I have built a career around policy, advocacy, and stakeholder engagement. At Venture Global Consulting, I helped develop legislative agendas, track legislation, engage lawmakers and staff, and organize Advocacy Days in Washington, DC. At CADCA, I built bipartisan support across congressional chambers and developed advocacy toolkits and legislative alerts used by more than 5,000 coalition members. Earlier, in the office of Congresswoman Roybal-Allard, I managed a health policy portfolio for an appropriator and coordinated partner engagement to inform legislative priorities.

I would also bring strong policy analysis and policy education experience to this role. At the Prenatal-to-Three Policy Impact Center at the LBJ School of Public Affairs, I conducted evidence reviews and policy research on issues including Medicaid expansion, group prenatal care, doula services, and screening programs for children. I translated those findings into technical briefs and annual reports for agencies and policymakers and responded to requests from legislative staff and partner organizations.

While my background has centered on health and prevention policy rather than housing policy specifically, the throughline has been consistent: helping organizations develop policy priorities, engage partners, and advocate for better outcomes for families and communities. Based in South Jersey, I am available for regular in-person work in Philadelphia and frequent travel to Harrisburg. After several years focused on full-time caregiving, I am eager to return to full-time policy leadership in a role where strong advocacy, coalition-building, and clear communication can support lasting systems change.

Thank you for your consideration. I would welcome the opportunity to discuss how my legislative, coalition, and policy research experience could support the Housing Alliance's agenda and help strengthen advocacy for safe and affordable housing across Pennsylvania.

Sincerely,

Nicole Semenza, LSW
