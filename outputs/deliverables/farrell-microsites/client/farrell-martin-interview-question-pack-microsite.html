Stratus + Cushman Interview Question Pack

Company-specific interview questions to pressure-test role clarity, authority, culture fit, and ownership risk before committing to next steps.

How To Use

  1. Ask Priority 1 questions first in each interview.
  2. Capture exact wording of answers.
  3. Score confidence immediately after each conversation.
Goal: avoid role-title optimism and verify real decision authority, leadership support, and business context.

Stratus: Director of Client Excellence

Priority 1: Role Clarity

  1. What are the top 3 outcomes this role must deliver in the first 90 days?
  2. Which KPIs define success (retention, expansion, margin, NPS, SLA)?
  3. What decisions can I make independently vs requiring approval?
  4. Does this role own process redesign or mainly execution?
  5. Why was this role opened now?

Priority 2: Leadership and Culture

  1. What has worked and failed with prior leaders in this lane?
  2. What recurring friction exists between sales, ops, and client teams?
  3. How would my direct leader describe expected operating style?
  4. Share an example where client excellence challenged assumptions and leadership supported it.

Priority 3: Ownership/Funding Risk

  1. What ownership or board priorities shape this role in next 12 months?
  2. Are cost or org changes expected that affect this function?
  3. Is this role long-term capability build or short-term account stabilization?

Priority 4: Commercial Impact

  1. Which client segments are highest risk right now and why?
  2. Where is revenue leakage occurring today?
  3. How is account planning done today, and what is missing?
  4. What does a clear executive-level win look like in 6 months?

Priority 5: Team and Resources

  1. What team/resources are assigned to this role on day one?
  2. What tooling/data access supports account visibility?
  3. What constraints should I assume (headcount, systems, change authority)?

Cushman: Client/Account Leadership Path

Priority 1: Scope and Expectations

  1. What core business problem is this role expected to solve?
  2. What measurable outcomes are required at 90 and 180 days?
  3. How is success weighted across retention, growth, client sentiment, and execution?
  4. How much influence does this role have over delivery teams?

Priority 2: Org Dynamics

  1. How do high performers get cross-functional decisions made here?
  2. Where does collaboration usually break down?
  3. What behavior is rewarded that is not obvious from org chart/title?
  4. If I ask teams what frustrates clients most, what would they say?

Priority 3: Strategic Context

  1. What leadership/ownership priorities are non-negotiable right now?
  2. Any upcoming reorgs or investment shifts that affect this role?
  3. How should this role balance margin protection with client outcomes?

Priority 4: Client Excellence Model

  1. How are client health signals tracked (leading + lagging)?
  2. What is the escalation path for at-risk clients?
  3. How does client feedback get converted into action?
  4. Where is the biggest delivery gap vs client expectation?

Priority 5: Career and Risk Controls

  1. What support does leadership provide when difficult decisions are required?
  2. Top reasons people in similar roles succeed or fail here?
  3. What does a realistic 12-month growth path look like?

End-Of-Interview Close-Out Questions

  1. Is there any concern about my fit that we should address directly?
  2. What are the exact next steps and timeline on your side?
  3. What additional information would be most useful for your decision?

Post-Interview Debrief (Fast)

  1. Top 3 positives
  2. Top 3 risks
  3. Role clarity score (1-5)
  4. Culture confidence score (1-5)
  5. Ownership/financial risk score (1-5)
  6. Decision: proceed, proceed with conditions, or pause
