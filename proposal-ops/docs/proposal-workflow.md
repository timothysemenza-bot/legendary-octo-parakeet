# Boss Key Pursuit OS - Opportunity and Proposal Workflow

## Full Lifecycle

1. Policy / procurement signal monitoring
2. Opportunity hypothesis
3. Qualification
4. Capture strategy
5. Solicitation intake
6. Compliance framing
7. Content planning
8. Drafting
9. Review
10. Submission
11. Archive / lessons learned

## What Each Stage Means

### 1. Policy / procurement signal monitoring
- budgets
- agendas
- legislation
- program changes
- rebid indicators

### 2. Opportunity hypothesis
- likely buying event
- target agency or account
- timing hypothesis
- service-line fit
- confidence framing

### 3. Qualification
- fit assessment
- bid/no-bid or pursue/do-not-pursue decision
- resource and strategic alignment

### 4. Capture strategy
- stakeholder map
- contractor and teaming choices
- positioning themes
- capture actions
- commercial assumptions

### 5. Solicitation intake
- formal RFP, RFQ, bid, or grant release
- intake and structured record creation

### 6. Compliance framing
- requirements extraction
- deadlines and response obligations
- matrix and ownership structure

### 7. Content planning
- response architecture
- section strategy
- review sequence

### 8. Drafting
- content development
- pricing coordination
- production control

### 9. Review
- Pink, Red, Gold, or similar review gates
- comments and issue resolution

### 10. Submission
- final package validation
- authorization and handoff

### 11. Archive / lessons learned
- result logging
- retrospective notes
- reusable artifact capture

## Current Implemented Slice

- Stage 1 is still primarily service-led and documentation-driven
- Stages 2-4 are actively implemented in the janitorial operating wedge
- Stage 5 is partially implemented through opportunity intake and pursuit handoff
- Stages 6-10 are partially implemented or planned across downstream proposal modules

## Janitorial Wedge Mapping

### Implemented today
- contract radar and rebid visibility
- opportunity creation from contracts
- contractor prospecting and touchpoints
- capture workbench intelligence, evidence, and actions
- commercials and readiness views

### Planned generalization
- reusable source and signal tracking for non-janitorial sectors
- broader opportunity forecasting support before contract records exist
