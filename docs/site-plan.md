Secondary nav or footer:

## Site objective
Reposition Boss Key from a downstream proposal/operator advisory brand into an early-lifecycle government opportunity intelligence and capture advisory firm.

Core site promise:
- Boss Key helps clients see and shape likely public-sector opportunities before procurements are formally posted.

## Homepage structure

### 1) Executive Summary / Hero
- Objective: explain the early-lifecycle differentiator in under 10 seconds
- Key message: policy intelligence + opportunity forecasting + capture strategy + proposal execution
- Proof elements:
  - two-partner model
  - sector focus
  - “not lobbying, not generic proposal consulting” line
- CTA: `Book an opportunity briefing`

### 2) Problem framing
- Objective: help the buyer self-identify
- Key message: most contractors enter too late, when the RFP is already shaping the timeline
- Buyer pains:
  - reactive pipeline
  - no pre-RFP visibility
  - weak connection between policy/funding shifts and real pursuit decisions

### 3) How Boss Key works
- Objective: make the service architecture intuitive
- Key message:
  1. Policy / Procurement Intelligence
  2. Opportunity Forecasting
  3. Capture Strategy
  4. Proposal Execution
- Include a simple lifecycle diagram

### 4) Offer structure
- Objective: make the first buying step clear
- Key message: start with the Opportunity Foresight Sprint, then expand into retainer, capture, and proposal support
- Proof elements:
  - 3-4 week sprint
  - outputs
  - who it is for

### 5) Who we help
- Objective: narrow fit and strengthen credibility
- Priority sectors:
  - facilities services
  - healthcare / human services
  - education support
  - municipal services
  - nonprofits
  - public-sector technology vendors

### 6) Credibility and trust
- Objective: reduce perceived risk
- Key message: one partner brings proposal/capture execution depth; the other brings policy/program intelligence depth
- Proof elements:
  - partner bios
  - sector familiarity
  - methodology language
  - ethical / non-lobbying boundary

### 7) CTA section
- Objective: make next step explicit and low-friction
- Flow:
  - introductory briefing
  - paid foresight sprint
  - retainer or engagement decision
- CTA label: `Book an opportunity briefing`

## Messaging rules
- Lead with earlier visibility and positioning advantage
- Use “government opportunity intelligence” and “capture advisory” language
- Make “not lobbying” explicit where helpful
- Avoid sounding like a SaaS product, lobbying shop, or generic bid-writing vendor

## CTA rules
- Primary CTA: `Book an opportunity briefing`
- Secondary CTA: `Request the Opportunity Foresight Sprint`
- Conversion path should always emphasize:
  - briefing
  - paid sprint
  - retainer / capture scope
