# How We Work: Operating Cadence Descriptions (5)
- Weekly Pursuit Checkpoint: We run a structured weekly review of active pursuits to confirm priority, resolve blockers, and assign next decisions with owners and dates.
- Go/No-Go Review Rhythm: We hold a standing decision review to evaluate fit, resource load, and win path before teams commit drafting capacity.
- Proposal Readiness QA Gates: We apply predefined quality gates at key milestones so teams catch risks early and reduce late-cycle rework.
- Leadership Visibility Loop: We deliver a weekly decision memo summarizing pursuit status, risks, escalations, and required executive actions.
- Monthly Performance Reset: We review metric movement, adjust pursuit criteria, and reset operating focus based on measurable outcomes and pipeline reality.
