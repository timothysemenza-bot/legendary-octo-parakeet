# Current Pain Pattern Variants (10)
- Opportunities enter the pipeline faster than your team can qualify them, so weak deals consume scarce proposal capacity.
- Go/no-go decisions are delayed or reversed, creating rework and late-cycle pressure.
- Proposal teams are producing volume, but leadership lacks clear visibility into which pursuits are actually winnable.
- Intake quality varies by owner, so pursuit priorities shift week to week without shared criteria.
- Teams are drafting before strategic decisions are locked, which drives avoidable revision cycles.
- High-value pursuits compete with low-probability bids, reducing focus on the deals that matter most.
- Leadership gets status updates, but not timely decision-ready insight tied to risk and capacity.
- Proposal milestones are met inconsistently because accountability and handoffs are not enforced.
- Cross-functional teams are involved, but ownership is fragmented and escalation paths are unclear.
- The pipeline looks active, but decision latency and execution drag are reducing conversion performance.
