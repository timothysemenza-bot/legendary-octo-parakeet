How Boss Key and The Editry Collective Can Partner for Growth

This blueprint frames a direct partnership between Boss Key and The Editry Collective, grounded in Editry's internal operating docs. The core idea: Boss Key leads business development and channel growth while Editry leads delivery systems and product execution.

Boss Key BD Lead Editry Pro Nest Notes / NestSignal Editry Shield Greater Atlanta GTM

Partnership Thesis

Where To Win Fast

Sell an annual B2B school/daycare SMS service with low implementation friction and high immediate perceived value.

Where To Build Moat

Convert institution trust into parent opt-ins, then expand into Editry Pro household and operations support.

Where To Expand Margin

Layer premium services and add-ons (emergency overrides, RSVP campaigns, continuity planning, security advisory).

Best-Fit Partnership Lanes

Lane 1: Education & Childcare Revenue Engine
  • Primary offer: Nest Notes annual plans ($399, $549, $599 tiers).
  • Initial market: Greater Atlanta daycare and early learning operators.
  • Boss Key role: outbound pipeline build, referral partner network, and school onboarding ops.
  • Editry role: product delivery, messaging templates, CRM routing, and parent opt-in experience.
Lane 2: Senior Living & Emergency Messaging
  • Primary offer: NestSignal pilot + family reassurance communications.
  • Target buyers: multi-site senior communities and regional operators.
  • Boss Key role: enterprise introductions, pilot structuring, and expansion contracts.
  • Editry role: secure SMS workflow, contact validation, reporting, and deployment templates.
Lane 3: Shield + Pro Hybrid Deals
  • Bundle physical security strategy, continuity planning, and communication tooling for SMB and facilities clients.
  • Use Editry Shield service SKUs as entry points: site assessments, BCP advisory, managed coordination.
  • Boss Key role: complex deal pursuit and multi-service packaging.
  • Editry role: service execution playbooks, compliance posture, and cross-team delivery cadence.
