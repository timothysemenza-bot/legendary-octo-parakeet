# Impact Screening

## Decision Rules
- Compare the strongest available stories before drafting the `PPIP`.
- Prefer the story with the best mix of leadership, senior stakeholder engagement, measurable before/after results, sustainability, and interview defensibility.
- Score conservatively when direct artifact access is unavailable.
- Reject or defer stories that are mostly team output, lack clear candidate ownership, or cannot be backed by personal recollection plus reference-verifiable facts.

## Scoring Rubric
- `1` = weak or mostly unsupported
- `3` = plausible but needs stronger evidence
- `5` = strong, specific, and defensible
