Mutual Understanding
Services does not include any other CPA services except what is expressly listed in this agreement. Any incident occurring
prior to the date of this engagement is not covered by our service. i.e. Fed/State notices for periods before this
agreement.
1. 
Our Fees are based upon client's representation as to transaction activity. Fees will be reevaluated 90 days from this
agreement
2. 
Expedited monthly processing and / or financial statement preparation is an automatic change order.  i.e. expedited for
bank refinancing or mortgage loans.   
3. 
Client agrees to CPA password protecting prior accounting months/years to avoid making changes to closed accounting
months.     Client agrees to use our chart of accounts; CPA will create/ merge / delete as needed.
4. 
Client is ultimately responsible for the company general ledger and will periodically review reports and provide CPA with
any needed changes. Client agrees to identify all non - business (personal) related transactions.
5. 
Client will code/fill in memo field for all written checks & communicate owner loans and owner investments.
6. 
We are not responsible for omissions or missing items (i.e. bank / credit card / loans accounts)  not previously agreed to in
our written agreement.
7. 
Client agrees to respond timely to firm periodic requests for additional bookkeeping information, clarifications & open
items.  Untimely responses (30 days late) are a change order to this engagement and will result in our firm filing an
automatic tax filing extension for the client. 
8. 
This arrangement does not include end of QBO service file transfer.  End of service subscription transfers and file transition
will be billed at separate billing rates.
9. 
Our services do not include discussions with third-parties such as attorneys, bankers, federal and state parties. Any
discussions with third parties will be billed at standard billing rates.
10. 
Client agrees to discuss with us before sending any financial statements to a third party in order to the put client best
interests forward.
11. 
Any changes to this agreement must be in writing and agreed to by both parties.  However, additional bank/credit
accounts/loans added after signing will be deemed as automatic client change orders and will result in an immediate fee
increase.
12. 
Our arrangement does not include time/work required for the sale of or dissolution of the business as these matters are
complex and engagements will be discussed in advance.
13. 
No refunds only credit towards completion of work.
14. 
In the event that the firm changes its form of ownership, consolidates, sells, and/or merges, you consent to the assignment
of your account and the transfer of your records.  
15. 
None of the above mentioned services can be relied upon to detect errors, irregularities, or illegal acts that may exist.
16. 
We reserve the right to stop work without client recourse from penalties if client fees are not current.
17. 
Either party may terminate upon 30 days written notice.
18. 
Tax Preparation
We will prepare your federal and state income tax returns from information which you will furnish to us. We will not audit
or otherwise verify the data you submit, although it may be necessary to ask you for clarification of some of the
information. We will furnish you with questionnaires and worksheets to guide you in gathering the necessary information.
Your use of such forms will assist in keeping the fee to a minimum.
1. 
It is your responsibility to provide all the information required for the preparation of complete and accurate returns. You
should retain all the documents, cancelled checks and other data that form the basis of income and deductions. These may
be necessary to prove the accuracy and completeness of the returns to a taxing authority. You have the final responsibility
for the income tax returns and, therefore, you should review them carefully before you sign them.
2. 
Our work in connection with the preparation of your income tax returns does not include any procedures designed to
discover defalcations and/or irregularities, should any exist. We will render such accounting and bookkeeping assistance
as determined to be necessary for preparation of the income tax returns.
3. 
The law provides various penalties that may be imposed when taxpayers understate their tax liability. If you would like
information on the amount or the circumstances of these penalties, please contact us.
4. 
In order to avoid any delays in preparing your return, we are asking you to take the time to review and sign the
Engagement Letter upon receipt.  The Tax Organizer should be returned as soon as possible and be accompanied with all
tax documents required to prepare your return.
