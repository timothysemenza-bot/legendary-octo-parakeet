## Stratus: Director of Client Excellence (Priority Question Set)

### Priority 1: Role Clarity and Authority
1. What are the top 3 outcomes this role must deliver in the first 90 days?
2. Which KPIs define success for this role (retention, expansion, margin, NPS, SLA performance)?
3. What decisions can I make independently versus what requires approval?
4. Does this role own process redesign and client governance rhythm, or only execution support?
5. Why was this role opened now?

### Priority 2: Leadership and Culture Fit
1. What has worked and not worked with prior leaders in this function?
2. What are the top 2 recurring friction points between sales, operations, and client teams?
3. How would my direct leader describe the operating style expected in this seat?
4. Can you share an example where client excellence challenged a major internal assumption and leadership supported it?

### Priority 3: Ownership/Funding Risk
1. What ownership or board priorities are shaping this role over the next 12 months?
2. Are there planned cost or org structure changes that affect this function?
3. Is this role being built for long-term capability, or to stabilize specific accounts quickly?

### Priority 4: Client Model and Commercial Impact
1. Which client segments are highest-risk right now and why?
2. Where is most revenue leakage happening today (service gaps, churn, scope misalignment, pricing pressure)?
3. How is account planning done today, and what is missing from current execution?
4. What would a “win” in 6 months look like at executive level?

### Priority 5: Team and Resource Reality
1. What team resources are assigned to this role on day 1?
2. What tooling/data access is available for account visibility?
3. Are there constraints I should assume in headcount, systems, or process change authority?

## Cushman: Client/Account Leadership Path (Priority Question Set)

### Priority 1: Scope and Expectations
1. What business problem is this role expected to solve that is currently not solved?
2. What measurable outcomes must be achieved in first 90 and 180 days?
3. How is success weighted between retention, growth, client sentiment, and operational consistency?
4. How much influence does this role have over cross-functional delivery teams?

### Priority 2: Org Dynamics and Culture
1. How do high-performing leaders here get decisions made across functions?
2. Where does collaboration typically break down today?
3. What behavior gets rewarded here that is not obvious from title or org chart?
4. If I ask the team what is frustrating clients most right now, what would they say?

### Priority 3: Strategic and Financial Context
1. What current strategic priorities are non-negotiable from leadership/ownership?
2. Are there upcoming changes (reorg, investment shifts, account concentration risk) that I should know now?
3. How does this role support margin protection while improving client outcomes?

### Priority 4: Client Excellence Operating Model
1. How are client health signals tracked today (leading and lagging indicators)?
2. What is the current escalation path for at-risk clients?
3. What process exists for converting client feedback into service or account strategy changes?
4. Where do you see the biggest gap between what clients expect and what teams deliver?

### Priority 5: Career and Risk Controls
1. What support does this role receive from leadership when difficult client or internal decisions are required?
2. What are the top reasons people in similar roles succeed or fail here?
3. What does a realistic 12-month growth path look like if performance is strong?

---

## Close-Out Questions (Use At End Of Either Interview)
1. Based on our conversation, is there any concern about my fit for this role that we should address directly?
2. What are the next steps and timeline from your side?
3. What additional information would be most helpful for your decision?
