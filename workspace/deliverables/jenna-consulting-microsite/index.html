Jenna Rosa: 90-Day Consulting Break-In Plan

A focused, outcomes-first path to move from leadership operations roles into a clear consulting offer with immediate market traction.

Prepared for: Jenna Rosa Date: March 4, 2026 Type: One-page action plan

Positioning Thesis

Start with one narrow lane where Jenna already has executive credibility: facilities and account-retention consulting for regulated pharma environments. Sell fixed outcomes first, then expand to broader strategic advisory after proof points.

Primary Action Items

1) Pick One Lane

  • Anchor on regulated operations + client retention.
  • Avoid broad "general consultant" messaging for now.

2) Productize Two Offers

  • Retention Diagnostic (2 weeks, fixed fee).
  • 90-Day Playbook Build (implementation roadmap).

3) Publish Proof Stories

  • Use three quantified wins from prior roles.
  • Format each as: problem, intervention, result.

4) Tighten LinkedIn for Buyers

  • Lead with consulting outcomes and buyer language.
  • Name target stakeholders: COO, VP Ops, Account Leaders.

5) Start Warm Outreach

  • 20 targeted outreach messages to former clients and partners.
  • Ask each contact for one intro tied to renewal or transition risk.

6) Use Pilot-First Pricing

  • Fixed-fee pilot for easy approval.
  • Expand to advisory retainer only after pilot outcomes.

90-Day Execution Timeline

Days 1-14 | Foundation Build
  • Finalize consulting lane, offer names, and deliverables.
  • Draft one-page service sheet and discovery call script.
  • Update LinkedIn headline and summary for consulting intent.
Days 15-45 | Pipeline Activation
  • Run warm outreach cadence and book discovery calls weekly.
  • Close at least one paid diagnostic engagement.
  • Capture client language to refine offers and messaging.
Days 46-90 | Proof + Expansion
  • Convert first pilot into case study with clear metrics.
  • Launch second offer with tighter scope and stronger positioning.
  • Add testimonial-based credibility assets for ongoing outreach.

Suggested Initial Offers

Offer A: Account Retention Diagnostic

2-week fixed engagement focused on identifying renewal risk, communication gaps, and operational friction.

  • Deliverables: risk map, top 5 interventions, executive readout.
  • Ideal buyer: account leader or operations executive under renewal pressure.

Offer B: Transition & Playbook Sprint

4-6 week sprint to design and implement a practical operating playbook for team handoff and service continuity.

  • Deliverables: operating playbook, role ownership map, 30-day execution sequence.
  • Ideal buyer: multi-site teams handling launch, transition, or growth.

Success Metrics (First 90 Days)

Pipeline

  • Qualified discovery calls: 8-12

Revenue

  • Paid pilots closed: 1-2

Credibility

  • Published case stories: 2+

Formal Request

Executive Coaching Partnership Invitation

Jenna, I would like to formally request the opportunity to serve as your executive coach as you build this consulting practice. I believe we can create faster momentum together by combining structured strategy, weekly accountability, and practical execution support.

If you are open to it, I would like to continue as your advisor and coaching partner through this transition and support you as an active client.
