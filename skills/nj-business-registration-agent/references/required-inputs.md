# Required Inputs (NJ Registration)

Use this list before drafting or filing.

## Entity and Name
- Legal business name.
- Alternative names to use if name clearance fails.
- Entity type (`llc`, `corporation`, `nonprofit`, `sole-proprietor`).
- Domestic NJ formation or foreign qualification.

## Addresses and Contacts
- Principal business address.
- Mailing address (if different).
- Registered agent name and NJ street address.
- Primary email and phone for filing notices.

## Owners and Management
- Owner/member/shareholder names.
- Manager/director names where required.
- Responsible party for tax registration.

## Tax and Operations
- Federal EIN status (existing or needed).
- Planned first revenue date.
- NAICS code.
- Whether employees will be hired.
- Whether taxable goods/services are sold.

## Filing and Compliance
- Preferred effective date.
- Fiscal year end month.
- Industry licensing needs (state board, city, county).
- Annual report reminder owner.
