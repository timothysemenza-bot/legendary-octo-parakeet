# Filing Playbook (New Jersey)

## 1) Choose Path

- `Domestic LLC/Corporation/Nonprofit`:
  - File formation with New Jersey Division of Revenue and Enterprise Services (DORES).
- `Foreign entity`:
  - File NJ foreign qualification and prepare home-state certificate of good standing.
- `Sole proprietor`:
  - Formation filing is generally not required at the state entity level.
  - Continue with tax registration and local trade-name/licensing checks.

## 2) Formation / Qualification

- Confirm name availability.
- File the appropriate NJ business filing.
- Save confirmation, filing receipt, and NJ business ID.

## 3) Tax Registration (`NJ-REG`)

- Register for required NJ tax accounts.
- If hiring employees, include employer registration details.
- If selling taxable goods/services, include sales-tax registration details.

## 4) Federal and Banking Basics

- Obtain EIN (if not already assigned).
- Open business bank account after state and EIN documentation are complete.

## 5) Local and Industry Licensing

- Check city/county business license requirements.
- Check profession-specific board or permit requirements.

## 6) Ongoing Compliance

- File NJ annual report on time each year.
- Maintain active registered agent information.
- Maintain tax filing cadence (sales tax, payroll, income/franchise as applicable).

## Execution Rules for the Agent

- Ask for missing required inputs before generating filing instructions.
- Do not fabricate filing fees or due dates when unknown.
- Flag any missing legal documents (operating agreement, bylaws, good standing certificate).
- Recommend user verification on official NJ and IRS portals before final submission.
