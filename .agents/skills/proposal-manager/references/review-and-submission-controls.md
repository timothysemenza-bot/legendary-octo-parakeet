# Review And Submission Controls

## Pink Review

Use Pink Review to test structure, compliance coverage, and message discipline.

Exit when:

- the outline is stable
- every evaluation factor is mapped to content
- major content gaps are logged with owners and due dates
- the team agrees on win themes and proof points

## Red Review

Use Red Review to test the draft as an evaluator would see it.

Exit when:

- the proposal answers the buyer's likely questions
- strengths and discriminators are explicit and evidenced
- the pricing narrative aligns with the technical approach
- major evaluator-facing defects are resolved or have named disposition owners

## Gold Review

Use Gold Review to test production and submission readiness.

Exit when:

- all final files and forms exist in the controlled package
- executive, contracts, and pricing approvals are captured
- filenames, bookmarks, signatures, and attachments are locked
- the submission path and contingency path are both confirmed

## Configuration Control

- Maintain one controlled production draft.
- Use consistent version names such as `YYYYMMDD-section-status`.
- Close the comment window before final production begins.
- Convert comments into an issue log with severity, owner, due date, and status.
- Record approval checkpoints instead of relying on memory or chat history.

## Final Submission Checks

Verify these items before submission:

- customer instructions, page limits, file types, and naming rules
- pricing files and narrative consistency
- signed forms, representations, certifications, and attachments
- past performance references, resumes, and org charts
- portal credentials, upload size limits, and confirmation capture
- final timestamp and responsible sender

## Escalation Rules

Escalate to leadership when:

- a required approval is blocked
- pricing changes after Gold Review
- the submission portal fails or is unavailable
- a material compliance defect is discovered after production freeze
- the team wants to waive a customer instruction without written rationale
