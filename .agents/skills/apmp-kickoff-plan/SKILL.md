# APMP Kickoff Plan

## Purpose

Prepare the minimum complete planning package needed to launch a proposal effort without premature drafting or rework.

## When to use it

- when the team is approaching kickoff or needs to refresh kickoff materials
- when the proposal management plan, outline, or schedule is weak or missing
- when a recurring run should assemble launch materials for writers, reviewers, and leads

## Required inputs

- opportunity name, customer, and due date if known
- proposal type or submission method if known
- current strategy notes, draft executive-summary inputs, or outline fragments
- known contributors, leads, or role placeholders

## Expected outputs

- kickoff packet
- proposal schedule and milestone plan
- responsibility matrix or role assignment sheet
- outline and content-plan handoff notes

## Step-by-step instructions

1. Read `docs/apmp-automation-reference-map.md`, then use the kickoff, proposal-management-plan, and content-plan sources listed there.
2. Confirm the run is late enough in the cycle to justify kickoff. If the plan is still too immature, say that and identify the missing planning materials.
3. Build or refresh the proposal management plan snapshot: opportunity overview, roles, milestones, review points, outline status, and logistics.
4. Generate a realistic backward schedule from the due date. Preserve planning time, review time, and production time.
5. Assemble a kickoff packet with the schedule, outline, requirements baseline status, writers’ guidance, and draft executive-summary direction.
6. Keep the package concise and operational so it can be reused at the actual kickoff meeting.
7. End with the next handoff: compliance/content planning or daily team management.
