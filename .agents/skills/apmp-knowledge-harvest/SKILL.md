# APMP Knowledge Harvest

## Purpose

Turn completed work into reusable knowledge, actionable lessons, and cleaner future runs.

## When to use it

- when a review cycle, submission, or major milestone just ended
- when reusable content, proof points, or checklists should be harvested from recent work
- when a recurring run should build the knowledge layer rather than new proposal content

## Required inputs

- the recent artifacts, notes, or review outputs to harvest from
- any customer or internal feedback
- existing templates, standards, or repositories that might need updating

## Expected outputs

- lessons-learned summary with actions
- harvested reusable objects or candidate assets
- repository or standards update recommendations
- next-run improvements for the automation pack or proposal workflow

## Step-by-step instructions

1. Read `docs/apmp-automation-reference-map.md`, then use the lessons-learned, knowledge-management, and collaboration-tool sources listed there.
2. Separate immediate lessons from reusable knowledge. Lessons become actions; reusable knowledge becomes a stored asset, template candidate, or standards update.
3. Capture lessons soon after the milestone while the experience is fresh. Focus on process, quality, and customer understanding rather than blame.
4. Harvest content in small reusable chunks where possible. Favor discrete objects over large undifferentiated files.
5. Recommend where the harvested knowledge belongs: template, standard, checklist, reusable content, or repository note.
6. If customer feedback exists, incorporate it without defensiveness and turn it into specific follow-up actions.
7. End with the next reuse path: template update, standards update, playbook note, or future-capture improvement.
