# Templates

This folder holds reusable starting points.

## Current contents

- `project/`: templates used for bootstrapping new work in `projects/`
- `proposals/`: reusable proposal snippets, positioning, and templates
- `sample-content/`: reusable sample content scaffolding

## Rules

- Keep templates generic and reusable.
- Do not store client-specific deliverables here.
- Move mature repeated patterns here once they stop being one-off work.
