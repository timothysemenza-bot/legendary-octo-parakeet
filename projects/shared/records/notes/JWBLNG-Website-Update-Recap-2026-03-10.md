JWBLNG Website Update Discussion
Date: March 10, 2026

Key Takeaways

- The current site structure is accomplishing the immediate phase-one goal: make it easy for people to understand JWBLNG, request access, join, view events, and donate.
- The gated member flow is working conceptually: a user submits name, email, and interest; Jess and Marcy receive the request; approval triggers welcome email plus community access.
- Jess wants the experience to stay simple. She explicitly preferred a straightforward hub for events, recordings/archive, and future communications over a social-style feed.
- Marcy and Jess want clearer language than Kajabi defaults like "product" and "community access."
- The donate flow should include brief context before sending people to Zeffy.
- The next major phase is not more page sprawl. It is structured automation: welcome sequence, event reminders, newsletter cadence, and possible post-event follow-up.

Phase-One Decisions

- Keep the site simple and focused on signup, events, and donation.
- Use Kajabi's built-in event flow rather than a separate Zoom workflow.
- Keep event information private behind the gated community flow.
- Continue using approval-based access for new members.
- Keep the homepage content concise, but expand it enough to explain the organization, benefits of membership, and how to join.

Requested Changes From Jess and Marcy

- Replace placeholder imagery with better-fit images.
- Fix the missing button text on the login/join gate.
- Simplify navigation and labels.
- Add or clarify an "About Us" section.
- Add clearer language around "what you get as a member."
- Rework the community area so upcoming events are primary, while recordings/archive are secondary.
- Consider a simpler information architecture such as:
  - Events
  - News / Newsletter
  - Recordings / Archive
  - Donate
  - About Us
- Remove or de-emphasize clutter such as leaderboard/feed elements if they are not central to how members will use the site.
- Add more context to the donation page before routing users to Zeffy.

Admin / Workflow Decisions

- Jess and Marcy currently receive join requests directly, though this may later be routed to `info@jubling.org` or role-based JWBLNG addresses once email routing is finalized.
- Approval is controlled by Kajabi tags:
  - `pending review` blocks access
  - `approved member` grants access and triggers the welcome sequence
- Kajabi can support automated email sequences and triggered communications.

Phase-Two Direction

- Define a set of automations to quote and implement separately.
- Likely candidates:
  - welcome email sequence
  - recurring event reminders
  - event registration reminders
  - monthly newsletter workflow
  - member spotlight workflow
  - post-event feedback / survey follow-up
- The recommendation from the call was to build phase two by iterating on what already exists rather than creating too many new branches at once.
