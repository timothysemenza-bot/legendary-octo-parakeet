# Interview Diligence Scorecard
Candidate: Farrell Martin  
Prepared: March 3, 2026  
Target companies in active flow: Stratus, Cushman

## Why This Scorecard
This is designed to reduce false positives in executive interviews, especially where title/scope sounds strong but culture, ownership pressure, or role authority are weak.

## Scoring Method
- Rate each section from `1` to `5`
- `1` = weak/high risk
- `3` = acceptable/unclear
- `5` = strong/low risk
- Add brief evidence line for each score

## Section 1: Role Clarity and Authority
1. Is the role outcome clearly defined for first 90 days?
2. Are metrics tied to business impact (retention, expansion, margin, client NPS), not activity only?
3. Is decision authority explicit (process, headcount, budget, tooling)?
4. Are cross-functional dependencies realistic and supported by leadership?

## Section 2: Culture and Leadership Reality
1. Does direct leadership style match your operating style?
2. Is the interview behavior consistent with how teams actually operate?
3. Do leaders discuss failures and lessons openly (signal of maturity)?
4. Is there evidence of trust and accountability, not politics or churn?

## Section 3: Ownership and Financial Context
1. Who owns/funds the company today (PE, public, strategic owner)?
2. What is the likely ownership timeline (entry year, expected exit window)?
3. Are cost-cutting pressures likely in the next 12-18 months?
4. Is this role a build role or a pre-exit optics role?

## Section 4: Client Model Fit
1. Does the client portfolio match your strengths in enterprise account development?
2. Is the company serious about client excellence/client success as a function?
3. Is revenue growth expected from strategic relationship depth or high-volume churn?
4. Is there real support for data/insight-driven account strategy?

## Section 5: Career Risk Controls
1. Any legal constraints to review (non-compete/non-solicit history)?
2. Any leadership overlap that creates known friction risk?
3. Does role success depend on one person/political sponsor?
4. Is there a credible path if org priorities shift in 6 months?

## Red Flags (Immediate Escalation)
1. Role was not posted and scope is vague.
2. Interviewers cannot define decision rights.
3. Metrics are activity-heavy and outcome-light.
4. Leadership avoids ownership/funding questions.
5. Major “transformation” language with no execution resources.

## Must-Ask Questions (Use in Interview)
1. What are the top 3 outcomes this role must deliver in 90 days?
2. What decisions can I make without additional approval?
3. What has caused prior leaders in this lane to fail?
4. How is client excellence measured today and where is it currently underperforming?
5. What ownership or board-level milestones are driving this role right now?
6. If I am successful, what changes in 12 months that are visible at executive level?

## Weighted Recommendation
- Role Clarity and Authority: `__ / 20`
- Culture and Leadership Reality: `__ / 20`
- Ownership and Financial Context: `__ / 20`
- Client Model Fit: `__ / 20`
- Career Risk Controls: `__ / 20`

Total: `__ / 100`

Decision guide:
- `80-100`: strong fit, proceed
- `65-79`: proceed with explicit risk mitigations
- `<65`: high risk, pause or decline
