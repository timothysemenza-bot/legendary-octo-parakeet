Six‑Month Strategic Roadmap

Proposed first-six-month roadmap for the Associate Director of Public Policy & Advocacy role at CurePSP.

Executive Summary

CurePSP has built meaningful advocacy momentum through leadership, community advocates, and people directly impacted by PSP, CBD, and MSA. This plan is designed to help carry that momentum into a more consistent federal policy rhythm while keeping CurePSP's people-first values visible in every step.

The approach is practical for a one-person launch: focus on federal work first, align with current advocacy priorities, keep close coordination with the Steering Committee and leadership, and create clear public-facing progress updates so the broader CurePSP community can stay informed and engaged.

What This Plan Is Built To Do

Honor and Extend Legislative Wins

  • Build on CurePSP's legislative wins to date like the National Plan to End Parkinson's Project.
  • Continue existing legislative priorities for affordable and accessible medical care.
  • Continue support for family care partners.
  • Continue work to improve diagnostics for PSP, CBD, and MSA.
  • Increase funding to better understand the etiology and/or epidemiology of PSP and CBD.

Center People and Care Partners

  • Bring people with lived experience forward early in strategy, not only at execution.
  • Keep care partners, support groups, and community advocates in regular feedback loops.
  • Use plain-language updates that show what is happening and why.

Create A Repeatable Policy Rhythm

  • Use a structured annual legislative calendar tied to the annual appropriations cycle.
  • Track meetings, follow-ups, coalition actions, and outcomes in one shared system.
  • Issue monthly updates to leadership and the Steering Committee.

Strengthen Transparency and Accountability

  • Publish regular policy progress snapshots for the broader CurePSP community.
  • Invite feedback early and continuously, including when tactics need adjustment.
  • Keep process documentation clear and transferable.

30/60/90/180-Day Implementation Roadmap

Window Main Focus Key Actions Outputs
Days 1-30 Ground the work in CurePSP context and current priorities
  • Meet with leadership, the Steering Committee, and internal partners to confirm priorities and expectations.
  • Review existing advocacy assets, coalition commitments, previous asks, and office relationships.
  • Set up a shared tracker for congressional outreach, follow-up actions, and progress notes.
  • Baseline memo: where the work stands now and immediate next actions.
  • Federal target framework and outreach calendar.
  • First monthly update format for leadership and Steering Committee.
Days 31-60 Launch federal outreach and coordinated partner engagement
  • Run first round of House and Senate meetings aligned to appropriations timing.
  • Coordinate asks and timing with coalition partners where CurePSP can add clear value.
  • Activate advocate and care partner outreach tools for district-level reinforcement.
  • Meeting briefs and office-level follow-up.
  • Community-facing policy update draft for website/newsletter use.
Days 61-90 Convert early traction into consistent progress
  • Run second outreach wave based on learning from first round.
  • Refine office prioritization criteria using response and engagement patterns.
  • Hold structured check-in with Steering Committee and leadership for adjustments.
  • Monthly policy dashboard with actions completed, outcomes, and next-month focus.
  • Updated congressional pathway map.
Days 91-180 Sustain federal cadence and expand priority relationships
  • Maintain federal appropriations and relationship-building cycle.
  • Run a focused second-phase outreach wave based on response patterns and committee activity.
  • Continue monthly internal and community update cadence.
  • Six-month impact summary and lessons learned.
  • Priority-office summary with recommendations for next steps.
  • Refined playbook for continued execution.

How Progress Will Be Reported

  • Monthly: Leadership + Steering Committee update (actions, outcomes, upcoming asks).
  • Monthly: Public-facing community update (brief website/newsletter format).
  • Quarterly: Expanded Board-ready policy summary.

Execution Standards For The First Six Months

1) Federal-First Discipline

  • Keep federal advocacy as the core focus in year one.
  • Keep secondary initiatives tightly scoped so they support, not distract from, federal progress.

2) People-Centered Policy Practice

  • Integrate people with lived experience and care partners into planning and outreach.
  • Ensure messaging reflects real patient and family realities, not abstract policy language.

3) Clear, Consistent Communication

  • Provide concise updates that leadership, board members, and community members can all follow.
  • Show what was done, what moved, what did not move yet, and what happens next.

4) Continuous Improvement Without Ego

  • Welcome course correction quickly when tactics are not landing.
  • Keep the focus on mission progress, not process defensiveness.

Immediate Next Steps If Selected

  1. Confirm first 30-day priorities with leadership and Steering Committee.
  2. Launch appropriations-focused outreach calendar and shared tracker.
  3. Publish first monthly update for both internal teams and community audiences.
  4. Finalize second-phase target list based on early outreach response and committee alignment.
