# Sample RFP Fragment

## Section L: Proposal Instructions

1. Offerors shall submit a technical proposal, management proposal, staffing plan, transition plan, past performance volume, and pricing volume.
2. Offerors shall follow the section numbering and titles used in this solicitation.
3. Offerors shall provide a transition approach for onboarding within 30 days of award.
4. Offerors shall describe how patient-facing staff will be trained, supervised, and evaluated.
5. Offerors shall provide three relevant past performance examples from healthcare or similarly regulated service environments.
6. Offerors shall identify key personnel and provide resumes for the proposed Program Manager and Site Leads.
7. Offerors shall submit all questions by the stated deadline through the sourcing portal.

## Section M: Evaluation Factors

- technical and management approach
- staffing and transition readiness
- relevant past performance
- price

## Implied hot buttons

- low disruption during transition
- visible service quality reporting
- confidence in patient-facing professionalism
- operational discipline across multiple locations
