# Sample Client Brief

## Client

Northstar Health Network

## Client type

Regional healthcare services organization with multiple outpatient sites and a growing need for facilities, patient-facing support, and proposal process standardization across vendor engagements.

## Why this is a good demo client

- complex enough to require structured pursuit and proposal management
- familiar enough that a prospective client can map the scenario to their own environment
- generic enough to avoid confidentiality concerns

## Business pain points

- pursuit decisions are inconsistent across business units
- kickoff materials are assembled late
- compliance control is manual and uneven
- reviews happen, but issue tracking and follow-up are inconsistent
- lessons learned are captured sporadically and are rarely reused

## Desired outcomes

- clearer gate discipline
- stronger kickoff readiness
- faster compliance and outline setup
- more structured review cycles
- reusable knowledge after each major bid event
