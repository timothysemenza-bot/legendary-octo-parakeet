## Annotated outline excerpt

### 1. Technical Approach
- answer the customer's need for reliable multi-site service coverage
- reinforce transition readiness and reporting visibility
- include one graphic showing service delivery model

### 2. Management Approach
- explain oversight structure, escalation path, and quality controls
- use customer terminology where possible
- add proof point from prior multi-site engagement

### 3. Staffing and Supervision
- directly answer training, supervision, and evaluation requirement
- include staffing governance and performance feedback loop
- call out patient-facing professionalism

### 4. Transition Plan
- structure around pre-award planning, day-one readiness, and 30-day stabilization
- include transition milestones and risk controls

## Author guidance

- preserve customer order and language
- start each major section by answering the requirement directly
- tie every claim to a proof point, example, or operational control
