## Contract Shape

- The RFP is designed to award four individual contracts, generally one contract per region.
- Regions 1-3 are detention-heavy and County-furnished for most custodial supplies.
- Region 4 is the best Boss Key pilot lane because it is a more conventional dispersed station portfolio with contractor-furnished supplies, public-lobby obligations, and lighter staffing footprints than the detention-heavy regions.
- Region 4 summary from the facility sheets:
  - 28 facilities
  - about 564,199 user square feet
  - about 849,880 gross square feet
  - mixed weekday, evening, and limited weekend staffing across stations and substations

## Evaluation Model

- Business Proposal: 60%
- Cost Proposal: 40%
- Labor law or payroll history can trigger an additional deduction of 1% to 10% of available evaluation points.
- The County may still select a proposal other than the highest-scoring proposal if it determines another is most qualified, responsive, responsible, and in the County's best interest.

## What The Buyer Is Really Buying

This is not a generic office-cleaning bid. LASD is buying custodial control inside a public-safety environment with labor-compliance scrutiny, security restrictions, background clearances, emergency responsiveness, and high consequence for visible misses in lobbies, restrooms, custody-side areas, and public-facing spaces.

For the Boss Key pilot, Region 4 is the cleanest test of whether the engine can tell a credible story about:

- multi-site operating control
- security-safe staffing discipline
- supply continuity across scattered locations
- rapid correction and callback response
- labor and healthcare compliance without looking bureaucratic

## Confirmed Requirements That Matter Early

- Minimum mandatory experience: two years within the last five years providing custodial services in office buildings, institutional facilities, or similar facilities
- Three references from different companies are required
- Business proposal must include executive summary, qualifications, approach, quality control plan, living wage compliance response, exception handling, forms, and corporate documents
- Cost proposal must include pricing schedule, budget sheet, and company information sheet
- Region 4 requires contractor-furnished supplies and maintenance of a one-to-two-week inventory at facilities
- Emergency calls must be returned within 30 minutes if voicemail answers, with a written cost estimate within four hours of County request
- Contractor must maintain a County office staffed during normal business hours with after-hours answering coverage
- Contractor employees are subject to background clearances and facility security rules, including no phones, cameras, or contraband

## Boss Key Pilot Position

Start with Region 4, not the full four-region package.

Why:

- It is still a serious public-sector janitorial pursuit, but the story is closer to a real dispersed janitorial operating model than Regions 1-3.
- It pressure-tests staffing, replenishment, public-facing quality, reporting, and response time without forcing the first Boss Key live run to center on the hardest custody-cleaning modules.
- It exposes where the current Boss Key schema is still private-sector biased, especially around contract value, start date, incumbent posture, and portfolio-level public procurement instructions.

## Working Assumptions

- Boss Key is using Region 4 as the first live pilot lane, not all four regions.
- Public-lobby cleanliness, supply continuity, and callback responsiveness will matter more to first-read evaluators than generic corporate branding language.
- Pricing pressure should be treated as high because cost carries 40% of the score and the RFP pushes detailed staffing, labor, and healthcare cost logic into the proposal.
