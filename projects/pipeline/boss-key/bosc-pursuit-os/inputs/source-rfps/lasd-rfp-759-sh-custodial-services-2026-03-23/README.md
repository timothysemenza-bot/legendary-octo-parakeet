# LASD RFP 759-SH Source Notes

## Why this packet

This is a live public-sector custodial services procurement from the Los Angeles County Sheriff's Department that was still listed as open on March 23, 2026.

- Solicitation: `RFP 759-SH - Custodial Services`
- Agency: Los Angeles County Sheriff's Department
- Official listing: `https://lasd.org/transparency/solicitations/`
- Date entered: January 22, 2026
- Proposal deadline: April 9, 2026 at 3:00 p.m. PT

## Why it is a strong BOSC test case

- Official public-sector source with no paywall
- Full packet is available, not just the cover RFP
- Includes revised bulletins and Q&A attachments
- Has real evaluation and compliance pressure, not only a pricing sheet
- Covers staffing, healthcare, living wage, references, required forms, and contract terms
- Includes facility and performance attachments that should pressure-test story generation against operations detail

## Important caveat

This is not a plain office-cleaning bid. It includes sheriff facilities and some region-specific detention-cell cleaning requirements, so it is a tougher and more security-heavy janitorial pursuit than a city hall or library contract.
