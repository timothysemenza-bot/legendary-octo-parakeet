# MVP Storyboard (5-Step Demo)

## Step 1: Intake

Upload or paste an RFP into `RFP Intake` and capture `rfp_submission` fields.

## Step 2: Asset Binding

Show client-specific `sharepoint_asset_index` loaded for brand files, approved claims, and template constraints.

## Step 3: Qualification

Present `qualification_result` output with weighted score, recommendation, rationale, and missing data.

## Step 4: Decision Discipline

Adjust gate checks and score sliders in `Go / No-Go Decision`, then save final recommendation.

## Step 5: Red-Team Draft

Generate `proposal_draft_v1`, show citations per section, add reviewer notes, and regenerate while preserving locked sections.

## Backup Flow

If live parsing fails, click `Seed Demo Data` and rerun Steps 3-5 on pre-validated sample data.
