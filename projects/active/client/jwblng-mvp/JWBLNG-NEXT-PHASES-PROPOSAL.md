# JWBLNG Website Next Steps Proposal
Date: 2026-03-17

## Overview
Phase 1 gave JWBLNG a strong foundation: a clearer public website, a simpler join path, and starter communications inside Kajabi.

This proposal outlines the next steps for building on that foundation in a way that is practical, budget-conscious, and aligned with the priorities discussed in the March 16 working session.

Rather than trying to fold every remaining idea into the initial phase, I recommend treating the next body of work as a separate paid engagement. That allows the website expansion, content work, and communications setup to receive the time and care they deserve.

## Phase 2: Website Expansion and Trust-Building
This phase focuses on making the public-facing website feel fuller, warmer, and more representative of JWBLNG.

### Included in Phase 2
- Selective migration of approved content and visuals from the existing Wix site
- A board or leadership section once bios and headshots are provided
- Testimonials or endorsements once approved copy is available
- Homepage and About page refinement to strengthen trust, clarity, and identity
- Blog, resources, and podcast landing structure so future content has a clear home
- Donation-page refinement if final check-by-mail details are provided
- Visual refresh using approved photos and imagery

### Timeline and Working Style
- Fixed project fee
- 3-4 week timeline
- One consolidated revision round
- JWBLNG provides missing assets, approvals, and final content inputs

### What Completion Looks Like
- Updated public pages are live
- Approved assets and messaging are in place
- Navigation remains simple and easy to follow

## Phase 3: Communications and Member Experience
This phase focuses on the systems behind the website so it is easier to manage member communications, support events, and prepare for future growth.

### Included in Phase 3
- Final sender/domain email readiness
- Cleanup and polish for key email campaigns
- Event reminder and follow-up workflow completion
- Refinement of the member approval and onboarding flow
- Clearer structure for future public and member-only content
- Setup for future classes, workshops, or paid offerings
- Basic analytics and reporting setup
- Optional cleanup of Kajabi community or feed areas, if needed

### Timeline and Working Style
- Fixed project fee
- 4-6 week timeline
- One consolidated revision round
- Focused on implementation and setup, not ongoing day-to-day management

### What Completion Looks Like
- Email campaigns are tested and ready to use
- Core automations and workflows are documented
- Member and event communications are easier to run
- The platform is better prepared for future programming and revenue opportunities

## Optional Monthly Support
After a project phase is complete, I can also provide limited monthly support for items such as:
- event updates
- email sends
- minor page edits
- advisory and prioritization support

This would be a capped monthly support arrangement, not an open-ended implementation bucket.

## My Recommendation
I recommend moving into Phase 2 first.

That will allow JWBLNG to strengthen the public website, bring in the most important additional content, and present a more complete and credible experience before investing in the deeper systems work covered in Phase 3.

Once Phase 2 is complete, it will be much easier to decide whether Phase 3 should happen immediately or later.

If budget is the main concern, the best adjustment is to narrow the Phase 2 deliverables rather than reduce the full project fee. That keeps the work clear and protects quality.

## What I Would Need From JWBLNG
- Board bios and headshots
- Approved testimonials or endorsement copy
- Approved images for the website refresh
- Final mailing details for check donations, if those should be published
- Timely review and approval of content and design decisions

Missing items would only affect the related deliverables, not the entire project.
