What Is Already In Place

  • Clear join path: a simpler public path into JWBLNG membership request and onboarding.
  • Core website pages: homepage, About, Contact, Events, Support, and three program pages.
  • Starter communications: welcome, reminder, and monthly digest templates.
  • Basic launch setup: one donation path, one standard event flow, and the operating basics needed to launch.
  • Handoff materials: supporting documentation, QA notes, launch checklist, and rollback checklist.

Final Phase 1 Closeout

  • Email testing: complete and record the remaining seed-test proof for key email assets.
  • Sender readiness: confirm sender/domain setup and note any outside dependency, if needed.
  • Handoff session: complete the walkthrough for approvals, events, and email operations.
  • Sign-off: confirm any deferred, asset-dependent items and formally close the phase.

Phase 2

Website Expansion and Trust-Building

  • Selected Wix migration: bring over approved copy and visuals that strengthen the public website.
  • Leadership and credibility: add a board or leadership section, plus testimonials or endorsements when approved materials are available.
  • Content homes: create the structure for blog, resources, and podcast content so future updates have a clear place to live.
  • Homepage and donation refinement: strengthen trust-building content and finalize donation details if mailing information is provided.
  • Visual refresh: update imagery using approved photos and references that reflect the community well.
Best next step if the priority is to make the public website feel more complete, welcoming, and representative of JWBLNG.

Phase 3

Communications and Member Experience

  • Email readiness: finalize sender/domain setup and polish key email campaigns.
  • Event and onboarding workflows: finish reminder flows and refine the member approval experience.
  • Content organization: create a clearer structure for future public and member-only content.
  • Future program setup: prepare Kajabi for future classes, workshops, or paid offerings.
  • Measurement and cleanup: add basic analytics and clean up Kajabi areas that need tightening.
Best follow-on phase if the priority is smoother communications, stronger member operations, and better preparation for future growth.

Why This Is Split Into Phases

  • Easier to budget: each phase has a clear scope, fee, and timeline.
  • Easier to prioritize: JWBLNG can move forward with the public website first, then decide what to do next.
  • Easier to manage: missing assets only affect the related deliverables instead of holding up everything.
  • Easier to sustain: monthly support is available later if ongoing help would be useful.
