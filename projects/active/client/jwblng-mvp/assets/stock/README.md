## Style guardrails

- Keep imagery realistic and documentary-like (not staged corporate stock feel).
- Prefer modest professional attire and respectful representation.
- Favor warm, human interactions over isolated office scenes.
- Avoid overly saturated colors that clash with JWBLNG purple palette.

## Basic crop targets

- Hero/portal images: 16:9-ish wide crops
- Program cards: 3:2 horizontal crops
- Member portraits: square crops
