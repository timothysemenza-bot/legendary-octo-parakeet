# Mutual Strategic Collaboration and Intellectual Property Protection Agreement

This Mutual Strategic Collaboration and Intellectual Property Protection Agreement ("Agreement") is entered into as of [Effective Date] by and between:

- **Service Performance, Inc. dba Revenue-IQ** ("Revenue-IQ"), a Washington corporation; and
- **Boss Key LLC** ("Boss Key"), a New Jersey limited liability company.

Revenue-IQ and Boss Key may each be referred to as a "Party" and together as the "Parties."

## 1. Purpose

The Parties wish to explore and, where mutually agreed, perform project work relating to AI-assisted proposal, capture, qualification, and related workflow services (the "Purpose").

This Agreement establishes the baseline rules for confidentiality, intellectual property, data handling, and relationship boundaries. Any project-specific scope, deliverables, commercials, acceptance criteria, timeline, and support obligations will be documented in one or more written statements of work signed by both Parties (each, an "SOW").

## 2. Relationship of the Parties

The Parties are independent contractors. Nothing in this Agreement creates a partnership, joint venture, fiduciary relationship, agency relationship, employment relationship, or exclusivity arrangement between the Parties.

Neither Party has authority to bind the other except as expressly set out in a signed writing.

## 3. Background IP

Each Party retains all right, title, and interest in and to its respective pre-existing and independently developed intellectual property, materials, methods, know-how, data, software, documentation, templates, prompts, workflows, systems, and tools ("Background IP").

For clarity, Boss Key Background IP includes Boss Key's software, source code, system architecture, prompts, prompt libraries, templates, schemas, connectors, automations, workflows, tests, evaluation assets, analytics, documentation, and generalized proposal/capture operating methods, whether existing before this Agreement or developed outside the use of Revenue-IQ Confidential Information.

For clarity, Revenue-IQ Background IP includes Revenue-IQ's proposal methodologies, proposal templates, sales frameworks, win-loss insights, business methods, training materials, and client-facing commercial materials developed outside the use of Boss Key Confidential Information.

Except for the limited rights expressly stated in this Agreement or a signed SOW, neither Party receives any ownership interest in the other Party's Background IP.

## 4. Limited Project License

During the Term, each Party grants the other a limited, non-exclusive, non-transferable, non-sublicensable license to use the other Party's Background IP solely to the extent reasonably necessary to perform an applicable SOW and only for the Purpose.

No Party may use the other Party's Background IP to build or commercialize a competing standalone product or service outside the scope of an applicable SOW without the other Party's prior written consent.

Nothing in this Agreement restricts either Party from using its own generalized skills, residual know-how, independently developed materials, or non-confidential lessons learned, so long as such use does not disclose the other Party's Confidential Information or infringe the other Party's Background IP.

## 5. Confidential Information

"Confidential Information" means any non-public business, technical, commercial, financial, client, or operational information disclosed by or on behalf of one Party to the other Party, whether in written, oral, visual, electronic, or other form, that is marked confidential or would reasonably be understood to be confidential under the circumstances.

Confidential Information does not include information that the receiving Party can demonstrate:

1. was publicly available at the time of disclosure or becomes public through no fault of the receiving Party;
2. was lawfully known by the receiving Party before disclosure by the disclosing Party;
3. is lawfully received from a third party without restriction; or
4. is independently developed without use of the disclosing Party's Confidential Information.

Each receiving Party will:

1. use Confidential Information only for the Purpose and applicable SOWs;
2. disclose Confidential Information only to personnel, contractors, legal counsel, accountants, and advisors with a need to know and who are bound by obligations at least as protective as this Agreement; and
3. protect Confidential Information using at least reasonable care.

## 6. AI and Data Handling

Neither Party may use the other Party's Confidential Information to train or fine-tune any public or shared AI model without the disclosing Party's prior written consent.

Project-specific model weights, retrieval indexes, or datasets created exclusively from one Party's Confidential Information for a signed SOW will be treated as project artifacts for that Party, subject to Section 8 below.

Notwithstanding the foregoing, each Party retains all rights in its Background IP, including its platform software, orchestration layer, prompts, evaluation assets, workflows, logs, backups, and de-identified performance telemetry, except to the extent expressly assigned in a signed SOW.

Any deployment, provider selection, retention settings, logging controls, approved or prohibited AI vendors, and security requirements for client-facing production use will be documented in the applicable SOW, security addendum, or both.

## 7. Return, Destruction, and Retention

Upon written request or upon termination of this Agreement, each Party will promptly return or destroy the other Party's Confidential Information in its active systems, except for:

1. archival copies retained in ordinary-course backups;
2. immutable logs and security records;
3. records required to be retained by law, tax, insurance, or professional-obligation requirements; and
4. routine internal copies necessary to document the Parties' commercial relationship.

Any retained materials remain subject to this Agreement.

Upon reasonable written request, the receiving Party will confirm completion of return or destruction in writing.

## 8. Ownership of Work Product and Deliverables

Each Party retains ownership of its Background IP and any tools, software, prompts, workflows, templates, schemas, libraries, documentation, and improvements used to perform the Services.

Unless otherwise stated in a signed SOW, final client-specific deliverables expressly created for Revenue-IQ or its designated client under an SOW, including client-ready proposals, reports, summaries, and related presentation materials, will belong to Revenue-IQ upon full payment of the fees due for the applicable SOW.

Boss Key retains ownership of all underlying Boss Key Background IP used to create those deliverables. Revenue-IQ receives an implied perpetual internal-use license to the extent necessary to use, reproduce, modify, and deliver the final paid-for deliverables for its business and client-service purposes.

No transfer of ownership applies to open-source software, third-party licensed materials, or pre-existing Party materials except to the extent expressly stated in a signed SOW.

## 9. Client Relationship Protection

The Parties acknowledge that introductions and trust-building efforts have value and should be respected.

Accordingly, during the Term and for twelve (12) months thereafter, Boss Key will not knowingly solicit, for substantially similar services, any client first introduced by Revenue-IQ and with whom Boss Key had direct material contact through work under an applicable SOW, except for:

1. pre-existing relationships known to Boss Key before the introduction;
2. responses to public solicitations or public procurement opportunities;
3. general marketing not specifically directed at such client; or
4. independent inbound opportunities not arising from misuse of Revenue-IQ Confidential Information.

If Revenue-IQ expressly authorizes a direct engagement with such an introduced client, the Parties may agree in writing to a referral or revenue-share arrangement for that engagement. Unless otherwise agreed in writing, any such referral fee will be a one-time fee tied to fees actually received from the initial engagement.

The same restrictions apply in reverse to Revenue-IQ with respect to clients first introduced by Boss Key through work under an applicable SOW.

## 10. Non-Solicitation of Personnel

During the Term and for twelve (12) months thereafter, neither Party will knowingly and directly solicit for employment any employee or long-term dedicated contractor of the other Party who was materially involved in the Parties' joint work, except through general recruiting not targeted at such person or in response to unsolicited inquiries.

If the Parties wish to attach any placement or transition fee to a specific personnel move, they will do so only in a separate written agreement.

## 11. Human Review and Use of Outputs

The Parties intend that AI-assisted outputs will be used under human supervision.

Revenue-IQ will remain responsible for final business, sales, proposal, and client-delivery review of materials delivered onward to Revenue-IQ clients unless a signed SOW expressly assigns a different approval role.

Boss Key will use commercially reasonable care in designing and operating any technical workflow performed under an SOW.

## 12. Indemnity

Each Party will indemnify, defend, and hold harmless the other Party from third-party claims arising from:

1. the indemnifying Party's gross negligence, willful misconduct, or knowing violation of law; or
2. the indemnifying Party's breach of its confidentiality obligations under this Agreement.

Boss Key will also defend third-party claims that deliverables created by Boss Key under an SOW infringe such third party's copyright or trade secret rights, but only to the extent the claim does not arise from:

1. Revenue-IQ materials, instructions, or data;
2. modifications by Revenue-IQ or third parties;
3. combinations with systems or content not provided by Boss Key;
4. use outside the scope of the SOW; or
5. third-party models, services, or tools required by Revenue-IQ.

Revenue-IQ will defend third-party claims arising from Revenue-IQ-provided materials, data, templates, claims, or instructions, including claims that Revenue-IQ lacked the right to provide or use them for the Purpose.

## 13. Limitation of Liability

Except for breaches of confidentiality, willful misconduct, gross negligence, payment obligations, or each Party's indemnity obligations, neither Party will be liable to the other for any indirect, incidental, special, consequential, exemplary, or punitive damages, including lost profits or lost business opportunity, even if advised of the possibility of such damages.

Except for breaches of confidentiality, willful misconduct, gross negligence, payment obligations, or each Party's indemnity obligations, each Party's aggregate liability arising out of this Agreement and all SOWs will not exceed the total amounts paid or payable under the applicable SOW giving rise to the claim during the twelve (12) months preceding the event giving rise to liability.

## 14. Term and Termination

This Agreement begins on the Effective Date and continues for one (1) year, automatically renewing for successive one-year periods unless either Party gives thirty (30) days' written notice of non-renewal.

Either Party may terminate this Agreement for convenience upon thirty (30) days' written notice, provided that any active SOW will continue under its own terms unless separately terminated in accordance with that SOW.

Either Party may terminate this Agreement immediately upon written notice if the other Party materially breaches this Agreement and fails to cure the breach within fifteen (15) days after written notice.

Sections that by their nature should survive termination will survive, including Sections 3, 5, 6, 7, 8, 9, 10, 12, 13, and 15.

## 15. Governing Law and Dispute Resolution

The Parties will first attempt in good faith to resolve any dispute through discussion between senior business principals.

If the dispute is not resolved informally, the Parties will participate in remote mediation before pursuing litigation, unless either Party seeks injunctive relief for breach of confidentiality or misuse of intellectual property.

This Agreement will be governed by the laws of [New Jersey / mutually agreed state], without regard to conflict-of-laws principles.

Any litigation relating to this Agreement will be brought in the state or federal courts located in [New Jersey / mutually agreed venue], and each Party consents to that jurisdiction and venue.

## 16. Miscellaneous

1. This Agreement is the entire agreement between the Parties regarding its subject matter and supersedes prior discussions on that subject.
2. Any amendment must be in writing and signed by both Parties.
3. Neither Party may assign this Agreement without the other Party's prior written consent, except to a successor in connection with merger, acquisition, or sale of substantially all assets.
4. If any provision is held unenforceable, the remaining provisions will remain in effect.
5. This Agreement may be signed in counterparts and by electronic signature.
