# Boss Key LLC - Menu of Services
## For St. Moritz Security Services, Inc.
**Prepared:** February 25, 2026

### 1) NDA + Security Scoping
**Outcome:** fast legal coverage and implementation guardrails.

- Mutual NDA execution support and scope alignment.
- Data handling map: intake, storage, retention, deletion.
- Access model definition: user roles, least-privilege, audit trail.
- Security constraints captured for pilot and production.

### 2) RFP Ingestion Pipeline
**Outcome:** reliable intake of every solicitation artifact.

- Intake from PDF, Word, email exports, and supplemental attachments.
- OCR processing for scanned documents where needed.
- Structured parsing into sections, requirements, dates, and artifacts.
- Version tracking so changes are visible across amendments/Q&A.

### 3) AI RFP Digest Engine
**Outcome:** decision-ready summary in hours, not days.

- Executive summary of scope, effort, and bid posture.
- Requirements matrix (mandatory vs. optional, owner, due date).
- Risk and gap log (compliance, staffing, pricing, technical).
- Win-theme extraction and response strategy prompts.
- Deadline tracker and critical path view.

### 4) Interactive Q&A Copilot
**Outcome:** teams can query the RFP corpus directly.

- Natural-language Q&A over full RFP package and amendments.
- Evidence-linked answers with source references.
- Role-tailored views for sales, legal, operations, and finance.
- Prompt guardrails to reduce hallucination and enforce citation.

### 5) Deployment + Compliance Options
**Outcome:** delivery model aligned to your security requirements.

- Client environment deployment options (`on-prem`, private cloud, VPC).
- Identity and access integration options.
- Logging, retention, and handoff controls for governance.
- Optional security addendum support for procurement/legal review.

### 6) Pilot to Production Delivery
**Outcome:** low-risk rollout with measurable ROI.

- **2-week pilot** on live or recent RFP documents.
- Weekly checkpoint cadence with stakeholder readouts.
- KPI tracking: time-to-digest, review effort, and issue detection.
- Production hardening plan, runbook, and ownership transition.

## Recommended Pilot Scope (2 Weeks)
- 1-2 recent or active RFPs
- 3-5 internal users across sales/legal/ops
- Deliverables:
  - Executive digest
  - Requirements/compliance matrix
  - Risk register
  - Q&A copilot workspace

## Information Needed to Launch
1. Data residency and hosting requirements.
2. Approved/prohibited AI models or vendors.
3. Typical RFP size, format mix, and monthly volume.
4. Required output formats (Word, Excel, slides, portal upload).
5. Review/approval workflow owners.
6. Target date for first usable pilot output.
