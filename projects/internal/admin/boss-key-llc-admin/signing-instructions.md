# Boss Key LLC Operating Agreement Signing Instructions

## Fastest (No Paid Subscription)

1. Open `projects/internal/admin/boss-key-llc-admin/operating-agreement-single-member-nj.md`.
2. Copy into Word or Google Docs and export as PDF named:
   `Boss-Key-LLC-Operating-Agreement-Signed.pdf` (after signature).
3. On iPhone:
   - Open PDF in Files.
   - Tap Markup (pen icon).
   - Add signature.
   - Place signature on signature line and save.

This is generally acceptable for internal governance and most bank onboarding.

## Adobe Acrobat (Optional)

If you prefer Adobe:

1. Open the PDF in Adobe Acrobat Reader.
2. Use Fill & Sign to place signature and date.
3. Save the signed copy.

Acrobat Pro is not required for a single-signature internal operating agreement.

## Recordkeeping

Store these files in one folder:

1. Signed operating agreement PDF
2. NJ formation confirmation/certificate
3. EIN confirmation letter (when issued)
4. Registered agent invoice/confirmation
