# Federal Search Playbook

Use this playbook after SAM registration is active.

Official references:

- [SAM.gov entity registration](https://sam.gov/entity-registration)
- [SBA prime and subcontracting guide](https://www.sba.gov/federal-contracting/contracting-guide/prime-subcontracting)
- [SBA Small Business Search](https://search.certifications.sba.gov)
- [SBA SUBNet](https://subnet.sba.gov)

## Goal

Build a repeatable federal pipeline around work Boss Key can credibly deliver now:

- management consulting
- proposal and capture support
- program support and PMO support
- process improvement
- solutions architecture
- portal, website, and application support work

## Search order

1. SAM.gov contract opportunities
2. SBA Small Business Search profile optimization
3. SBA SUBNet subcontracting opportunities
4. Agency small-business and procurement forecast pages
5. Prime-contractor outreach where large businesses carry subcontracting-plan obligations

## Target NAICS

- `541611`
- `541618`
- `541512`
- `541511`
- `541519`

## Include keywords

- `management consulting`
- `program support`
- `PMO`
- `business process`
- `process improvement`
- `operations support`
- `digital transformation`
- `IT modernization`
- `requirements analysis`
- `systems design`
- `solutions architecture`
- `web development`
- `portal`
- `content management`
- `application support`
- `user experience`
- `workflow`
- `proposal support`

## Exclude or down-rank

- construction and A/E work
- commodity buys and equipment-only procurements
- labor-only staffing without an advisory or delivery edge
- opportunities that require cleared defense performance on day one
- opportunities restricted to a certification Boss Key does not hold
- product-build scopes that expect a large engineering bench from day one

## Search filters to favor

- `Set-Aside = Small Business`
- `Set-Aside = Total Small Business`
- unrestricted notices that are realistic subcontracting targets
- advisory, support, modernization, portal, or process-heavy scopes
- opportunities where the government values responsiveness, planning, compliance, documentation, and coordination

## Early target buyers

- HUD and housing-program support offices
- HHS and health-program support buyers
- civilian agencies buying program support, PMO, modernization, and digital-service support
- transit and infrastructure agencies when the scope is planning, digital, or program-support oriented
- federal primes that need small-business teammates for operations, proposal, and implementation support

## Triage rules

- Bid when the scope matches Boss Key's service lane and the set-aside rules are actually open to the company.
- Hold when the scope is close but needs a teammate, domain specialist, or stronger technical bench.
- No-bid when the scope is construction, deep engineering, commodity delivery, or restricted to a certification Boss Key does not hold.
- No-bid when security clearance, mandatory past performance, or compliance burdens clearly outrun current capacity.

## Weekly cadence

- Monday: run saved SAM searches and capture every plausible lead.
- Tuesday: review amendments, question deadlines, and mandatory registrations.
- Wednesday: review subcontracting leads in SUBNet and prime-contractor directories.
- Thursday: make bid, hold, or no-bid decisions and write a one-paragraph reason.
- Friday: update pipeline notes and outreach list for the next week.

## Working rule

Boss Key should bias toward opportunities where sharp qualification, structured delivery, stakeholder coordination, and clean written work matter more than sheer headcount.
