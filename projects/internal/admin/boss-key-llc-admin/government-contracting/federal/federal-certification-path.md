# Federal Certification Path

There is no single federal certification that works as the equivalent of a state small-business certificate.

For Boss Key, the base federal path is:

1. be small under the right NAICS codes
2. register in SAM.gov
3. complete the SBA profile
4. pursue small-business set-asides and subcontracting

After that, only add extra certifications if Boss Key actually qualifies.

## Immediate path

- Treat `small business` status under the right NAICS codes as the first unlock.
- Do not wait for a second certification to begin searching federal opportunities.
- Start both prime and subcontracting pursuit once SAM is active.

## Candidate programs to evaluate

### Small Disadvantaged Business

- Register through SAM if the firm is at least 51% owned and controlled by one or more socially and economically disadvantaged individuals.
- Good program to evaluate early if it is factually true.
- Official reference: [SBA Small Disadvantaged Business](https://www.sba.gov/federal-contracting/contracting-assistance-programs/small-disadvantaged-business)

### Women-Owned Small Business

- Evaluate only if the firm is at least 51% owned and controlled by one or more women and meets SBA program requirements.
- Official reference: [SBA WOSB program](https://www.sba.gov/federal-contracting/contracting-assistance-programs/women-owned-small-business-federal-contract-program)

### Veteran contracting programs and VetCert

- Evaluate only if the ownership and control facts support veteran or service-disabled veteran status.
- Official reference: [SBA veteran contracting assistance](https://www.sba.gov/federal-contracting/contracting-assistance-programs/veteran-contracting-assistance-programs)

### HUBZone

- Evaluate only if the principal office and employee-residency rules can be met.
- This can be powerful, but it is location-and-workforce-specific.
- Official reference: [SBA HUBZone program](https://www.sba.gov/federal-contracting/contracting-assistance-programs/hubzone-program)

### 8(a) Business Development

- Treat this as a later decision, not a day-one dependency.
- SBA's normal path includes a two-year-in-business expectation, subject to waiver rules.
- Official reference: [SBA 8(a) program](https://www.sba.gov/federal-contracting/contracting-assistance-programs/8a-business-development-program)

## Boss Key recommendation

- Start with plain small-business eligibility plus strong SAM and SBA profiles.
- Evaluate SDB, WOSB, VetCert, and HUBZone only if the ownership and location facts support them.
- Do not delay federal market entry waiting for a certification that may not apply.

## Questions to answer before choosing a certification path

- Is the firm at least 51% owned and controlled by a woman?
- Is the firm at least 51% owned and controlled by a qualifying veteran or service-disabled veteran?
- Does the principal office sit in a HUBZone, and can workforce-residency rules be satisfied?
- Does the ownership structure support SDB representations?
- Has the business met the time-in-business requirements for 8(a), or is there a credible waiver case?
