# First-Wave Prime Target Outreach List

Prepared: `2026-03-18`

Use this list to guide prime registration and teaming outreach while SAM validation is pending and immediately after the UEI is assigned.

## Working posture

- Lead with `management consulting`, `proposal support`, `PMO/program support`, `process improvement`, and `service-led digital support`.
- Keep `solutions architecture`, `portal support`, and `web/app support` in scope as secondary lanes.
- Prefer primes with strong civilian, health, human-services, modernization, program-support, and professional-services volume.
- Treat defense-heavy or clearance-heavy partners as secondary unless a specific no-clearance civilian path is visible.

## Rank 1: Accenture Federal Services

- Why it fits: Accenture Federal Services says it works with every category of small and minority-owned business partner, performs more than 20 percent of its government contracts with small business partners, and maintains an online registration path for future teaming opportunities.
- Best Boss Key angle: civilian consulting, proposal support, PMO support, workflow improvement, and digital-service support for modernization efforts.
- Route: [Accenture Federal Services small business engagement](https://www.accenture.com/in-en/support/us-federal-government/small-business-services-overview)
- Timing: `Start now`
- Reach-out goal: submit the registration form with a one-page capability statement and Boss Key's consulting-first narrative.

## Rank 2: Maximus Federal

- Why it fits: Maximus Federal runs a Small Business and Strategic Partnership Office and explicitly invites `Small Business (SB)` firms into its network. Its federal posture is especially relevant to citizen services, health, and program-delivery support.
- Best Boss Key angle: program support, operating-model cleanup, proposal support, process improvement, and service-led digital support for public-facing programs.
- Route: [Maximus Federal small business network](https://maximus.com/federal-government/small-business-and-strategic-partnership-office.html)
- Timing: `Start now`
- Reach-out goal: join the network and position Boss Key as a responsive operations and delivery teammate for civilian and health-oriented work.

## Rank 3: GDIT

- Why it fits: GDIT says small businesses help it deliver `technology and professional services`, gives a clear `Research -> Register -> Be Proactive` path, and recommends that suppliers complete both `SAM` and `MySBA` information thoroughly.
- Best Boss Key angle: digital modernization support, portal and application support, PMO/program support, requirements translation, and process-improvement work in civilian or health lanes.
- Route: [GDIT Small Business Partners and Suppliers Center](https://www.gdit.com/about-gdit/doing-business-with-gdit/small-business-partners-suppliers/)
- Registration path: [GDIT ESRP registration page](https://www.gdit.com/about-gdit/doing-business-with-gdit/small-business-partners-suppliers/register/)
- Timing: `Prep now, submit after UEI`
- Reach-out goal: finish the capability one-pager now, then complete ESRP as soon as the UEI is available and the SBA profile is ready.

## Rank 4: Booz Allen

- Why it fits: Booz Allen says it routinely seeks small businesses to supplement its `professional, technical, and support capabilities`, provides a business-profile registration path, and says it subcontracted over `$1 billion` in fiscal year 2020, with more than `66 percent` going to small businesses.
- Best Boss Key angle: management consulting, expert services, proposal support, process improvement, digital solutions support, and civilian mission delivery.
- Route: [Booz Allen and Small Businesses](https://www.boozallen.com/menu/government-contract-vehicles/subcontractors.html)
- Registration path: [Booz Allen suppliers page](https://www.boozallen.com/menu/suppliers-and-small-businesses.html)
- Timing: `Start now`
- Reach-out goal: register Boss Key in the supplier database and follow with a short intro tied to a specific civilian or modernization lane.

## Rank 5: SAIC

- Why it fits: SAIC highlights `Professional Services` and `Program Management` among its focus areas and says it works with select companies on strategic small-business set-aside pursuits. It also makes clear that teaming activity happens well before the RFP stage.
- Best Boss Key angle: program support, professional services, PMO support, proposal support, digital transformation support, and niche consulting support on civilian or federal-health pursuits.
- Route: [SAIC suppliers and small business page](https://experience.saic.com/suppliers-and-small-business)
- Timing: `Queue for after UEI and one-pager`
- Reach-out goal: approach SAIC only after Boss Key can present a tight line card, NAICS list, and a clear target-opportunity angle.

## Rank 6: Leidos

- Why it fits: Leidos says `small business is good business`, keeps a profile database for small-business capabilities and past performance, and provides a process for small subcontractors to request past-performance evaluations. It is a strong long-term fit for digital-service, modernization, and mission-support lanes.
- Best Boss Key angle: modernization support, portal and app support, process improvement, proposal support, and program-support work where Leidos needs a smaller operator on a civilian or health program.
- Route: [Leidos suppliers page](https://www.leidos.com/suppliers)
- Timing: `Queue for targeted outreach after UEI`
- Reach-out goal: do not do blind portal-only outreach. Identify a specific program or growth team, then present Boss Key as a niche support teammate with a focused capability one-pager.

## Rank 7: Peraton

- Why it fits: Peraton says more than `54 percent` of its subcontracted services and procured products go to small business and provides a prospective supplier capability form for partnership intake.
- Best Boss Key angle: program support, digital modernization support, workflow/process support, and targeted civilian or mission-service subcontract roles.
- Route: [Peraton supplier partnerships](https://www.peraton.com/suppliers/supplier-partnerships/)
- Intake form: [Peraton prospective supplier capability form](https://www.peraton.com/suppliers/prospective-supplier-intake-form)
- Timing: `Wait for UEI and ideally CAGE`
- Reach-out goal: use Peraton after the full SAM registration is farther along because the capability form asks for `UEI`, `CAGE`, `NAICS`, and a capability statement.

## First execution order

1. Use `boss-key-federal-capability-statement-2026-03-18.pdf` as the first-wave capability attachment.
2. Register with `Accenture Federal Services` and `Maximus Federal`.
3. Prepare `GDIT` materials now and submit after UEI assignment.
4. Register Boss Key with `Booz Allen` once the one-pager is ready.
5. Queue `SAIC` and `Leidos` for targeted, opportunity-led outreach.
6. Use `Peraton` after UEI and CAGE are available.

## Messaging guardrails

- Lead with what Boss Key can deliver now, not what may be true later.
- Do not claim federal certifications that are not active.
- Do not overstate past performance.
- Keep the pitch centered on structured delivery, strong written work, compliance discipline, coordination, and service-led digital support.
- Use the same NAICS and positioning language across SAM, SBA, and prime registrations.
