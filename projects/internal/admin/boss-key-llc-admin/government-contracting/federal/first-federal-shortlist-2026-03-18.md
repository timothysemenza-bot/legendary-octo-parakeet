## First-pass takeaway

- SAM.gov is the strongest near-term source for direct-fit federal leads.
- SUBNet is still worth a weekly scan, but the current visible inventory is not as aligned to Boss Key's consulting-first lane.
- Prime outreach should start with capability-statement-led registration and introductions to civilian-focused large contractors, not with cold pursuit of mismatched field-work listings.
