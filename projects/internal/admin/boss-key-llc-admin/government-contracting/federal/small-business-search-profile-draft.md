# Small Business Search Profile Draft

Use this as the starting point for the SBA supplemental page and the public-facing capability narrative inside SAM and SBA Small Business Search.

## Positioning statement

Boss Key LLC is a small business focused on management consulting, proposal support, process improvement, PMO support, and digital-service support for public-sector and procurement-facing work. The firm is best positioned for advisory, coordination, modernization, portal, and workflow-oriented engagements where clarity, discipline, and structured delivery matter.

## Short capability summary

Boss Key supports organizations that need stronger pursuit discipline, cleaner operating processes, clearer program coordination, and practical digital-service support. The firm can contribute in management consulting, bid and proposal support, business-process improvement, PMO support, solutions architecture support, and web or portal support work where the engagement is service-led and execution-focused.

- Do not overstate past performance.
- Do not lead with janitorial or facilities work when marketing federal consulting and digital-service capabilities.
- Keep the language service-oriented, specific, and credible.
