# NJ SBE Search Playbook

## Goal

Use the NJ SBE certification to drive disciplined, repeatable pursuit of New Jersey state and local consulting opportunities that match Boss Key's actual service lane.

Federal expansion materials now live in `./federal/`. Use those documents to prepare SAM registration, evaluate federal certification paths, and stand up a federal search lane once the registration is active.

## Source Order

1. NJSTART manual review, CSV export, or API feed when available
2. Agency, authority, county, municipal, school, and public-college procurement pages when they publish outside NJSTART
3. Follow-on SAM review only after SAM, UEI, and CAGE readiness is documented under admin

## Target Buyer Classes

- State departments and state-level authorities
- Counties and municipalities
- Public schools, colleges, and universities
- Transit, healthcare, and facilities-adjacent public buyers when the scope is advisory or support-oriented

## Include Keywords

- `management consulting`
- `administrative consulting`
- `strategic planning`
- `process improvement`
- `business process`
- `workflow`
- `PMO support`
- `program support`
- `proposal management`
- `proposal support`
- `bid support`
- `compliance review`
- `RFP support`
- `technical/proposal writing`
- `digital transformation`

## Exclude Keywords

- `construction`
- `engineering`
- `HVAC`
- `plumbing`
- `commodity supply`
- `equipment purchase`
- `hardware`
- `janitorial self-performance`
- `custodial staffing`
- `snow removal`
- `landscaping`
- `software build`

## Qualification Rules

- Prioritize NAICS `541611`, `541618`, and selective `541512` opportunities when the scope is advisory instead of implementation-heavy.
- Treat NJ SBE or small-business-only language as positive when it fits the current certification lane.
- Fail qualification when the solicitation is reserved for a certification Boss Key does not hold, such as `WOSB`, `8(a)`, `SDVOSB`, `MWBE`, or similar restricted categories.
- Look for NJ commodity and NIGP references that support consulting work, especially `91875`, `91890`, and `91806`.
- Mark low fit when the scope is construction, facilities self-performance, commodity supply, equipment purchase, or labor-heavy operational delivery.

## Search Cadence

- Monday: run the three saved cockpit searches and capture every plausible opportunity into the cockpit.
- Wednesday: revisit open notices, amendments, and question deadlines; update gates and bid/no-bid calls.
- Friday: export the weekly cockpit report and note which opportunities move to pursue, hold, or no-bid.

## Working Rule

If the work reads like consulting, coordination, planning, qualification, PMO, proposal support, or process improvement, it stays in play. If it reads like physical delivery, staffing, commodity purchase, or a certification lane Boss Key does not hold, screen it out early.
