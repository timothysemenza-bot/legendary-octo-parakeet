# Boss Key Target Lanes

## Positioning

Boss Key's phase-1 government-contracting lane is direct consulting and support work, not prime self-performance of janitorial, facilities, construction, or commodity delivery. The strongest fit comes from the proposal-management profile, the public services positioning, and the NJ SBE certification now on file.

## Primary Lanes

- Proposal management: live RFP support, proposal planning, compliance matrices, review cycles, submission discipline, and cross-functional coordination.
- Bid analysis and qualification: go/no-go support, opportunity triage, capture framing, qualification memos, and pursuit decision support.
- Proposal operations improvement: workflow cleanup, content operations, response process design, governance, and proposal-library discipline.
- Administrative and general management consulting: management consulting, advisory support, operating rhythm design, and business-process structuring.
- Strategic planning: planning support, operating-model improvement, growth-process cleanup, and decision framing for procurement-facing teams.
- Process improvement: workflow redesign, business-process improvement, PMO support, and digital-transformation advisory when the work is not custom software build.
- PMO and program support: coordination, reporting rhythm, operating cadence, and support roles adjacent to implementation rather than labor-heavy service delivery.
- Pursuit and market watch support: watchlists, opportunity visibility, pipeline discipline, and early-stage procurement monitoring that leads into qualified bids.

## Best-Fit Buyers

- New Jersey state agencies and authorities
- Counties and municipalities
- School districts and public colleges
- Quasi-public healthcare, facilities, and institutional buyers when the scope is advisory, planning, or support work

## Low-Fit Work To Screen Out

- Construction, engineering, HVAC, plumbing, and site-work delivery
- Commodity buys, equipment purchases, uniforms, and supply contracts
- Janitorial or custodial self-performance as the prime contractor
- Software build projects where the deliverable is custom product development instead of advisory or process support
