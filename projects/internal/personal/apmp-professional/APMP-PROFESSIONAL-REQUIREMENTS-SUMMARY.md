# APMP Professional Requirements Summary

This file is a text aid distilled from the APMP source packet in this folder. Use it together with the original source files, not instead of them.

## Source Packet
- `APMP Professional Standards and Candidate Guidance Version 1.7.pdf`

- `APMP Professional Impact Self Scoring Matrix Version 1.5.docx`
- `APMP CV Template.docx`

## Eligibility Prerequisites
- Active APMP membership.
- APMP Practitioner or Capture Practitioner certification.
- Minimum 7 years of experience in a bid and proposals environment.
- One named reference who can comment on leadership, communication, and behavior/attitude competencies.

## Submission Package
- Proposal Professional Impact Paper (`PPIP`) slide deck.
- CV.
- Completed reference questionnaire.
- Candidate should self-score the `PPIP` before submission.

## PPIP Format Rules
- Use the APMP template sequence. Headings may be extended with a short qualifier, but their order should not change.
- Final deck must contain 7 to 13 slides including the APMP-branded front cover slide.
- Remove prompt text from the final slides.
- Summary of Impact, `STAR`, and `CPD` sections each need at least one slide with a graphic. If a section has only one slide, that slide must contain a graphic.
- Graphics include tables and need:
  - a figure number
  - an informative title
  - an action caption that adds meaning instead of repeating slide text

## PPIP Content Sections
- Cover
- Summary of impact
- Situation
- Tasks
- Activities
- Results
- `CPD` plan for the next 24 months

## PPIP Self-Scoring
- Assessors use a 12-criterion matrix scored `0` to `4`.
- Passing threshold for the slide submission is `41/48` or higher.

| # | Requirement | Strong evidence should show |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 1 | Followed the APMP template | Correct section order, 7 to 13 slides, prompt text removed, all required content present |
| 2 | Impact summary is complete | What the impact set out to achieve, what it achieved, timeframe, senior stakeholders, additional stakeholders |
| 3 | Senior stakeholder engagement throughout | Stakeholders named, initial buy-in, continued management, stakeholder management treated as a task, visible timeline/cadence |
| 4 | Timeline for tasks and activities | Clear graphic, lengths of tasks and activities, recurring actions, milestones/reviews, effort or team size |
| 5 | Tasks have aligned activities | Each task has supporting activities, enough activities to achieve the task, accountability is clear |
| 6 | Tasks and activities are specific | Specific, measurable, achievable, results-oriented, and time-bound tasks and activities |
| 7 | Quantitative and or qualitative results | At least two meaningful improvements, before/after baselines, measured outcomes, no unsupported claims |
| 8 | Results were measured | Method of measurement, frequency of measurement, review cadence, how findings are applied |
| 9 | Results are sustainable | Ongoing monitoring, lessons learned loop, not reliant on one person, documented changes, stakeholder support to evolve |
| 10 | `CPD` plan is specific | 24-month timeline, named learning activities, benefit to candidate, knowledge-sharing plan |
| 11 | Good balance of text and graphics | At least one graphic per section, concise bullets, balanced visuals and words |
| 12 | Graphics have meaningful action captions | Simple visuals, figure number, informative title, action caption |

## Interview Format
- Two APMP-approved assessors conduct the interview.
- Interview is a maximum of 1 hour.
- Candidate presents the `PPIP` for 20 minutes.
- Candidate then answers competency-based questions.
- Candidate is pre-notified of the competency questions and gets at least 5 days to prepare.

## Interview Scoring
- Presentation pass mark: `31/36`.
- Competency interview pass mark: `17/20`.
- Interview answers are judged on whether `STAR` is clearly described:
  - `Situation`
  - `Task`
  - `Action`
  - `Result`

## Likely Competency Areas
- Persuasiveness
- Communication
- Building relationships and teams
- Decision making and delegating responsibility
- Innovation
- Meeting leadership
- Continuous Professional Development

## Reference Questionnaire
- One reference scores 9 behavior and attitude competencies on a `1` to `6` scale.
- A strong submission should prepare evidence that supports `5` to `6` style ratings where possible.
- If the reference raises concerns, assessors may ask about them in the interview.
- If the reference is too weak, the candidate may be allowed to submit a different reference once.

## Candidate Workflow Implications
- The strongest `PPIP` story is one where the candidate personally led change through communication and stakeholder management.
- Measured before/after evidence is critical. Unsupported claims such as better branding or generic standardization are weak.
- Sustainability must be explicit, not implied.
- The same anchor story should support the `PPIP`, reference prep, and interview bank unless evidence clearly requires a different example for a specific competency.
