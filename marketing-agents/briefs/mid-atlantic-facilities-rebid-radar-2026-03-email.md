## March 2026 signals

1. Spring budget hardening
- Operating-line assumptions for custodial, EVS, and maintenance scopes are becoming easier to read before formal bid calendars publish.
- Action: map the accounts where board or budget hearings are converging now.

2. Summer reset planning
- School and campus environments often reveal scope change before the procurement office issues a notice.
- Action: watch committee agendas, summer planning language, and operating complaints that hint at service adjustments.

3. Contract anniversaries plus board calendars
- Renewal approvals, amendments, and anniversary timing still expose where a buyer may be stabilizing scope or setting up the next buying event.
- Action: build a simple account calendar instead of waiting for procurement portals alone.

4. Labor volatility
- Staffing pressure can push buyers toward scope rebasing, stronger transition expectations, and tighter performance controls.
- Action: prepare defensible notes on staffing model, transition cadence, and coverage logic now.

5. Cooperative purchasing pressure
- Some buyers may use shortcut vehicles or piggyback options instead of a full open bid.
- Action: track adjacent vehicles and public references that show how the target prefers to buy.
