# Stop having the owner run the proposal

Audience: Timmy personal profile
Status: Pending owner approval before publish/send.

## Primary draft
Owners, you should still run the business - but not the proposal.
When owners double as proposal managers, three things happen:
1) Opportunity visibility is late or fuzzy.
2) Pricing and staffing handoffs are rushed at the last minute.
3) Compliance and past-performance details get patched together.
I've seen capable firms lose purely from avoidable process failures.
You don't need a fancy team - just a repeatable capture-to-proposal handoff and one accountable operator.
