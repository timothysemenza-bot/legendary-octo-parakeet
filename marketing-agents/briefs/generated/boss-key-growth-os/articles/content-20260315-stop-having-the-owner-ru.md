# Stop having the owner run the proposal

## Summary
The strongest angle: owners running proposals create predictable weak points - late pricing/staffing handoffs, compliance gaps, and rushed decisions. Position the owner as necessary leader but not the day-to-day proposal manager; replace chaos with a simple capture-to-proposal discipline.

## Why this matters
The strongest angle: owners running proposals create predictable weak points - late pricing/staffing handoffs, compliance gaps, and rushed decisions. Position the owner as necessary leader but not the day-to-day proposal manager; replace chaos with a simple capture-to-proposal discipline.

## What I keep seeing
- When owners double as proposal managers, three things happen:
- Opportunity visibility is late or fuzzy.
- Pricing and staffing handoffs are rushed at the last minute.
- Compliance and past-performance details get patched together.

## What to do next
- Separate owner judgment from day-to-day proposal coordination.
- Put one accountable operator on the handoff between capture, pricing, staffing, and compliance.
- Use a short diagnostic or checklist before the next rebid or proposal cycle.
