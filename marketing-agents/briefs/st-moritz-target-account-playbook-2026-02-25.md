# Target Account Playbook (Model-Aligned)
Prepared: February 25, 2026

Source list:
- `marketing-agents/briefs/st-moritz-target-account-list-2026-02-25.csv`

## Revenue-model objective (annual)
- `4` Managed Retainers (`$6,000/mo`) = `$288,000`
- `1` Light Advisory (`$2,500/mo`) = `$30,000`
- `6` Pilot Sprints (`$5,500`) = `$33,000`
- Total = `$351,000`

## List structure
- `P1` accounts: highest probability Managed candidates (go first)
- `P2` accounts: strong Managed or Light candidates
- `P3` accounts: pilot-led enterprise wedge accounts

## Outreach sequence per account
1. Connect with primary buyer (`VP Sales`, `Business Development`, or `Proposal leadership`).
2. Send a one-paragraph pain hypothesis tied to active RFP burden.
3. Offer a fixed-price pilot (`$5,500`) with explicit deliverables.
4. On close, pre-wire conversion path to Managed (`$6,000/mo`) before pilot kickoff.

## Weekly operating cadence
- New accounts touched: `10-15`
- Discovery calls booked: `4-6`
- Pilot proposals sent: `2-3`
- Pilot closes target: `1 every 3-4 weeks`
- Managed conversion target from pilots: `40-60%`

## Pipeline math (practical)
- To land `4` Managed clients, plan for `8-10` pilot closes.
- At a `50%` pilot-to-managed conversion rate, this supports model goals with room for churn.
- Keep one Light Advisory slot for a high-fit but lower-volume client rather than discounting Managed scope.

## Non-negotiables in conversations
- Fixed commercial terms (avoid custom pricing early).
- Explicit service limits and overage terms.
- Start date commitment and named stakeholder owner.
- Written option selection confirmation before kickoff.
