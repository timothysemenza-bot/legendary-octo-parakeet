# Boss Key Post-Call Follow-Up Template

Use within 2 hours of call end.

## Subject Options

- `Great speaking today - next steps`
- `Recap and recommended next step`
- `Call recap: priorities and path forward`

## Core Follow-Up (Base Template)

Hi {{first_name}},

Thanks again for the conversation today.

Here is my understanding of your priorities:
- {{priority_1}}
- {{priority_2}}
- {{priority_3}}

Main bottleneck we identified:
- {{primary_bottleneck}}

If this remains unchanged, likely impact over the next 90 days:
- {{cost_of_inaction}}

Recommended next step:
- {{recommended_step}}

If that works for you, confirm by reply and I will send the short kickoff outline.

Timmy

---

## Variant A: Business Team Fit

Insert under `Recommended next step`:
- `Start with a focused diagnostic to map your current pursuit/proposal workflow, isolate the top operational blockers, and define a practical 30/60/90-day execution plan.`

Optional close:
`If helpful, I can include a simple owner-facing dashboard format so progress is easy to review weekly.`

## Variant B: Individual Seller Fit

Insert under `Recommended next step`:
- `Start with a seller diagnostic to audit stage flow, qualification discipline, and follow-up cadence, then move into weekly coaching focused on live opportunities.`

Optional close:
`This is designed to improve deal control and raise close consistency without adding admin overhead.`

## Variant C: Not A Fit Yet (Goodwill)

Hi {{first_name}},

Thanks for the conversation today.

Based on where things stand, I do not think a full engagement is the best next move right now.

Two practical actions I recommend immediately:
1. {{action_1}}
2. {{action_2}}

If priorities shift, I am happy to reconnect.

Timmy

## Internal Checklist Before Send

- Subject line clear and practical
- 3 bullets max in recap
- Next step is specific
- No pricing in follow-up unless asked
- Send within 2 hours
