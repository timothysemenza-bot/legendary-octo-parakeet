# Boss Key 20-Minute Discovery Call Script

Use this for both audiences:
- Business owners/leadership teams
- Individual sellers

Goal:
- Confirm fit quickly
- Surface the highest-value bottleneck
- Book a clear next step

## 0:00-1:30 | Opening

Script:
`Thanks for making time. I will keep this tight. I want to understand where your business development process is slowing down, what that is costing you, and whether I can help you fix it fast.`

Set expectation:
`If there is a fit, I will outline a practical next step at the end.`

## 1:30-4:00 | Context Snapshot

Ask:
1. `What are you focused on improving right now in business development?`
2. `What changed recently that made this a priority now?`

Capture:
- Current growth pressure
- Trigger event
- Decision urgency

## 4:00-10:00 | Bottleneck Diagnosis

For businesses:
1. `Where does work stall most often: qualification, pursuit, proposal, or follow-up?`
2. `What part of that process still runs manually every week?`
3. `Where do opportunities slip that should have moved forward?`

For individual sellers:
1. `At what stage do your deals slow down most often?`
2. `Where are you spending effort that is not converting?`
3. `What part of your follow-up process feels least consistent?`

Quantify:
- `How many opportunities per month are affected?`
- `Roughly what is that worth in pipeline or close rate impact?`

## 10:00-14:00 | Operational + Financial Impact

Ask:
1. `If nothing changes in the next 90 days, what is the likely cost?`
2. `If this gets fixed, what specific result matters most: speed, conversion, or time back?`

Translate to outcomes:
- Faster pursuit/proposal output
- Better qualification discipline
- Lower admin drag
- Higher close consistency

## 14:00-17:00 | Fit + Scope Check

Ask:
1. `Who besides you is involved in deciding to move forward?`
2. `What timeline would make this valuable to start?`
3. `Would you prefer a short diagnostic first or a direct retainer plan?`

Disqualify quickly if needed:
- No urgency
- No ownership
- No willingness to change process

## 17:00-20:00 | Close + Next Step

If fit:
`Based on what you shared, I recommend we start with a focused diagnostic. I will map the current flow, identify the top bottlenecks, and give you a practical action plan. If you want, we can then roll into ongoing support.`

Close question:
`Are you open to that as the next step?`

Then book:
- Date/time for next meeting
- Who should attend
- What they should bring (pipeline view, sample proposal flow, or current seller cadence notes)

If not fit:
`I do not think a full engagement is the best move yet. If helpful, I can send two practical recommendations you can apply immediately.`
