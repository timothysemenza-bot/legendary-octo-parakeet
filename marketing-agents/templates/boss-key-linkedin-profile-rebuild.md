# Boss Key LinkedIn Profile Rebuild
Date: 2026-03-13
Owner: Boss Key LLC

## Goal

Make Timmy's LinkedIn profile do four things fast:
- explain the niche
- prove the credibility
- show the offer ladder
- give warm traffic one clear next step

This profile should feel like an operator-advisor profile, not a generic consultant page.

## Profile direction

Use Timmy as the visible expert.

Do not position the profile like:
- founder helping everyone with business growth
- generic proposal writer
- vague fractional operations consultant

Do position it like:
- facilities and public-sector pursuit advisor
- proposal and capture operator
- person who helps capable contractors tighten qualification, visibility, and handoffs
