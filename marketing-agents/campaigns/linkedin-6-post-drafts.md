## Post 3: Tactical (Fri Feb 27)

Before you invest heavy proposal time, run these 3 checks:

1. Access: Do we have real access to decision-makers?  
2. Path: Is the buying path clear and active?  
3. Timing: Is the timeline credible enough to prioritize now?

If one is weak, pause and re-qualify before burning cycles.

This one habit alone can protect a lot of wasted effort.
